How many battery-electric buses should we order for the Niittylä depot's 2027 weekday blocks, and which blocks should they take, when most of those blocks have never been run by an electric bus? For every weekday block I need an estimate of its diesel use over the last twelve months in litres per 100 scheduled kilometres, a forecast from our own operating records of the battery energy in kWh a 2027 bus would use on it on our electrification standard's design day, and whether that forecast qualifies under the standard; I also want an estimate of the share of usable battery our electric buses lose for good in their first year, and a forecast of the energy in MWh the charging installation would draw at the connection point on the design night with the bus of every qualifying block on charge. A workbook with the block figures and a few slides for the board.
