Which of the Värnamo duties on our 2027 shortlist can a battery-electric tractor take over under our electrification standard, and what size of tractor order does that come to? Each shortlisted duty needs the battery energy its round trip will use on the January design day, forecast from what our Jönköping pilot tractors drew on their own duties, and a yes or no on going electric. I also want to know how much energy one tonne of payload adds per 100 loaded km and what share of its usable battery a tractor loses for good in its first year in service. The duties can go in a spreadsheet and the order in a one-page PDF memo. Energies to the nearest 10 kWh, the payload figure to 0.1 kWh and the loss to 0.1 per cent.
