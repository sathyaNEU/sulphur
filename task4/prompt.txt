Which part numbers will be short of spares in 2027, which are over-stocked, and how many spares should each one hold? A spreadsheet should give every part number's expected unscheduled removals in 2027 with an 80 per cent range, the spares it needs to keep our 95 per cent fill rate and the change against what the pool holds today, and a few slides should name the parts to buy and the parts to cut with what moves each of them, say how many 2027 removals scrapping every serial on the October watch list would save, and say which of those serials you would scrap.
