## Final Recommendation

Certify Garrow, Kittredge and Ostrom for the 2027 Community Transition Fund: on the records of 15 February 2027 their December 2026 covered jobs are an estimated 3.1, 2.7 and 2.9 per cent below December 2025, no other county is within 0.9 of a point of the 2.0 per cent line (Bray, the nearest, is at -1.0) and the state is at +0.1 per cent.
