## Forks run (Gate C)

1. **Trial reduction, schedule vs. recorded.** The regulator log shows some ON days stay in LOCAL/local mode or ramp over the first hour (confirmed above), so a day's nominal 4% schedule and its recorded output diverge on real rows. The ask itself ("per 1 percent of voltage removed") ties the coefficient to the volts actually removed, and VO-1 3.1 defines feeder-head voltage as the SCADA/regulator record, not the day's label. The schedule-based reading (0.53, named in Step 2) is ruled out by that wording and that record, not by a semantic toss-up: it answers a different quantity (response per ON-day assignment) than the one asked. Closed.
2. **Weather terms and control group.** Only one weather series is shipped (KGRI hourly temperature), so degree-hour terms are the only weather construction the files support; no second variable to choose between. The 8 trial feeders span three of the four classes (Residential, Commercial, Rural; HOL-116 and ELC-116/ELC-219/GBR-219 cover Residential and Rural), so "same-class untested feeders" is answerable from the register; sampled check of register classes confirms every trial feeder's class has multiple untested same-class peers. No second weather file or alternative class scheme is in the package to fork on.
3. **Heating split, resistive vs. heat pump.** residential_appliance_survey_2025.csv carries primary_heating per respondent per feeder (538 resistance, 461 heat pump, 1,283 gas, sampled), so each feeder's split is a filed fact, not an assumed constant; the annual report's load-research draws (11,700 kWh resistance, 4,200 kWh heat pump) are also filed. Closed.
4. **Losses: current at nominal kV vs. the feeder's own voltage.** The blocks' own Step 7 flags this as a shortcut and shows it adds ASH-113, ASH-219 and CDB-116 to the funded list. Checked the physics directly: fixing voltage at nominal while the whole exercise (VO-1 2.2, "the feeder's 2026 load... as it would have been drawn under VO") is to model load under a *lowered* voltage makes the current-under-VO ratio drop from ((1-s)/(1-dv))^2 (a rise) to (1-s)^2 (a fall) -- the sign of the loss effect flips, not just its size. That is self-contradictory with the stated purpose of the calculation (modeling a voltage-dependent quantity while holding voltage fixed), not a second defensible engineering convention. Ruled out by VO-1 2.2's wording plus the physics, not by a document sentence alone, but closed.
5. **No-load (intercept) term in the loss fit.** Refit loss (substation meter minus deduplicated MDM, 15-min) against current-squared with and without an intercept on all 11 head-metered feeders: the intercept is 1.2% to 11% of fitted loss depending on feeder (CDB-116 2.9%, HOL-222 11%), and dropping it moves the fitted slope by under 2% on every feeder checked. For the three near-breakeven unfunded feeders, this is far short of the swing needed to cross their break-even (recomputed threshold: ASH-113 needs +66 MWh, CDB-116 +74 MWh, HOL-219 +67 MWh on the loss side alone; the intercept's own-feeder magnitude is a few MWh). Does not flip the call. Moot.
6. **Trial ON hours restored vs. dropped in the yearly average.** VO-1 2.2 states directly: "The 2026 trial's ON days are not part of normal running." That sentence forces exactly the blocks' choice (restore to a full-year hypothetical, don't blend ON and OFF days into one blended average); there is no filed alternative reading. Closed.
7. **Margin-robustness on the three feeders nearest the line.** Recomputed break-evens (685.9 / 709.0 / 599.1) against the blocks' own stated SQ4 bands (593-668 / 609-684 / 508-571): every one of the three unfunded near-line feeders has its own stated upper band below its break-even by 2.6%-4.7%, and every one of the funded near-line feeders (ELC-113, ELC-219, GBR-113) clears its break-even by 9%-13% even at the low end of its band. The gap is thin but the blocks' own uncertainty band, computed jointly (not a naive recombination of marginal bounds on SQ5/SQ6/SQ7/SQ9, which is not a valid test here since those rows are correlated draws of the same regression), does not reach the line on any of the six named alternative computations in Step 7 except the nominal-kV shortcut, which is closed under (4).
