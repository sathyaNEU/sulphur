## Final Recommendation

Fund **insulation and heating controls** in the 2026 round and **not heating optimisation**: priced at the saving the 2019 lottery showed for each application's own building (controls at how much of the week it stands empty, read off its 2025 load profile; insulation at how much roof it has per square metre of floor, from the solar cadastre and the register) and carried over each measure's life, the applications received come to €108.74, €103.79 and €196.03 of grant per lifetime tonne of CO2 against the €120 limit (insulation, controls, heating optimisation). Pricing each application at its own use's saving (€132.06 and €128.67) funds nothing and is rejected, because this year's controls applications stand empty 68.15% of the week against 38.38% for the pilot's controls buildings and this year's insulation applications have 1.13 square metres of roof per square metre of floor against 0.79 (applications weighted by reference consumption, the pilot's buildings by their 2018/19 gas), and each measure's saving rises with them.

## Critical Components

1. Heating optimisation's saving falls from **12.27%** in its first winter to **1.74%** by the fourth and settles towards about 0.69%, so over its 15-year life it saves **2.12%** of the pilot buildings' gas, not the seven seasons' average of 3.75%.
2. Heating controls save nothing until about **40.5%** of the week is empty and then about 3.06 points more for each further tenth: **2.91%** of the gas at half the week empty, **5.96%** at three fifths and **9.02%** at seven tenths, and 1.46% in hotels and care homes lived in round the clock; this year's controls applications stand empty **68.15%** of the week (weighted by reference consumption), the pilot's controls buildings 38.38% (weighted by their 2018/19 gas, those lived in round the clock at none).
3. Roof insulation saves **23.98%** of a building's heating where the roof is as large as the floor, **15.04%** at half and **6.11%** at a quarter; this year's insulation applications have **1.13** square metres of roof per square metre of floor (weighted by reference consumption), the pilot's insulation buildings 0.79 (weighted by their 2018/19 gas).
4. Once the offer closes the funded round's applications ask for **€11,451,661** and it pays **€10,759,494** once the works are done, the late insulation applications larger (27.83% in 2019) and more often withdrawn.

3. Carried each measure's seasons over its life (plan 4.4 lives 30, 15 and 10 years) by fitting the approach to the level the saving settles at: heating optimisation's life saving is 2.12%, not the seven-season 3.75%; carried flat, heating optimisation would be €101.94 a tonne and wrongly funded.
4. Read each building's week off electricity_load_profiles_2025.csv (its market locations summed under its customer number in buildings_register_2026.xlsx): the hours whose load is under half-way from the week's lowest hour to its highest are empty, none where the load never falls below three quarters of its highest hour (in use round the clock); every hotel and care home is lived in round the clock, its guests or residents there at night, so its night and midday dips are sleep and quiet, not an empty building (read off their load alone the pilot's hotels and care homes stand over half the week empty, yet they saved 1.46% from controls). No other conclusion survives. The before and after funds all three on savings the comparison shows every building made; the pilot's seven seasons carried flat fund heating optimisation on winters it no longer saves; each use's saving, or the pilot's own mix, prices this year's controls and insulation applications at buildings that stand empty less and have less roof than theirs and funds nothing; carrying only one of the two drivers funds only that measure; and every defensible variant (four or eight bands, the bands as steps, the empty week read at 40 or 60 per cent of the load's range, every hotel and care home taken as lived in, insulation on its share of the gas, a two-season baseline, the last seasons carried) funds insulation and heating controls and leaves heating optimisation over the limit.
