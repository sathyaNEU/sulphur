How much credit write-off can the business support unit keep off Ruahine Bank's books through the watch-list business borrowers it takes on within its 2027 envelope? That choice and its total should head a workbook, bsu_intake_2027.xlsx, which also gives, for each borrower taken on, the intake we would fall back on were it to turn its package down and the 2027 write-off that intake would keep off. A second sheet should give the intake and the 2027 write-off it keeps off at every envelope from NZ$4.5 million to NZ$6.5 million, in steps of NZ$500,000. A note for the credit committee, bsu_intake_2027.docx, carries the reasoning. Write-offs are in New Zealand dollars to the nearest 50,000.
