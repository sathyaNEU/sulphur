# task3 submission: Port Tolliver's FY2028 tide valves

## Tags

**Domain:** Engineering and Physical Sciences, Physical & Environmental Sciences (extreme-value and environmental-hazard analysis: tidal high-water exceedance carried from a tide gauge to low points with short records).
**Analytical objective:** Forecasting & Predictive Modeling.

## Final Recommendation

**Fund the valves at Wharf Street at Market Lane and Front Street at Ferry Lane.** They expect 32.3 and 26.3 tidal flood days in the 2027-28 tide year against 15.9 at River Road at Hensley Landing, 14.3 at Marsh Road at Pellam Creek and 1.2 at Oyster Creek Road; this rejects the Marsh Road and River Road pair that straight-line campaign transfers give (33.6 and 36.7 days), because Pellam Creek's high water bends below the line once the tide tops the marsh and River Road's only campaign sat in a wet late winter whose river lift the autumn king tides do not get.

## Critical Components

1. Marsh Road floods at a harbor high water of **8.75 ft**, not the 8.54 ft one straight line through its 2024 campaign gives: Pellam Creek follows the harbor at 1.02 ft per foot below about 7.43 ft and 0.61 above it, so the road flooded on **17** days in 2025-26 (not 26) and expects **14.3** in 2027-28 (not 33.6).
2. River Road's high waters stand about **0.47 ft** higher at 25,000 cfs than at 5,000 cfs, and its campaign ran at 24,600 cfs on average, so carried day by day on the Brayle's flows it flooded on **10** days in 2025-26 (the campaign's single offset gives 26) and expects **15.9** in 2027-28 (not 36.7).
3. Front Street's lane sits at **5.19 ft NAVD88**, from the high tides that put water under its street sensor; it flooded on **21** days in 2025-26, 15 of them recorded by the sensor and 6 in its outage.
4. Expected 2027-28 days put Wharf Street at Market Lane (**32.3**) and Front Street at Ferry Lane (**26.3**) ahead of River Road at Hensley Landing (15.9), Marsh Road at Pellam Creek (14.3) and Oyster Creek Road (1.2), and the pair holds in **30 of 36** weather years.

## Step-by-Step

1. Put the harbor's verified high waters, predictions and monthly means (8673718 files) on the 1983-2001 MLLW datum and the inventory and loggers on NAVD88 with the datums page's 3.42 ft; each past tide year's mean sea level stands 0.63 ft above the epoch's in 2025-26.
2. Took each past high water's departure from its prediction less its tide year's mean sea level (a hourly residual swings through the tide, and the raw departure carries the sea level twice: 38.9 days); replayed on 2025-26 it gives the observed 21 days.
3. Ran the 2027-28 predicted high waters at the planning sea level (0.70 ft) under each of the 36 weather years: the harbor expects 27.0 days, 22 to 31 in nine years of ten; the count trend files 10.8 and a Poisson range 19 to 36.
4. Split the harbor's change from 21 to 27.0 over the six orders of applying sea level, tides and weather: +5.8, -0.7 and +1.0; taken one at a time from 2025-26 the three do not add to the change.
5. Read Front Street's lane from the street sensor (pt-front-01): water depth on the lane against the harbor high water at each wet tide gives 5.19 ft NAVD88 once single-reading vehicle spikes and rain ponding at lower tides are set aside; the drain rim in the inventory files 44 days for 2025-26, and the sensor alone misses its outage's 6.
6. Paired the 2024 Marsh Road campaign's (PEL-MR-24) high waters with the harbor's: one straight line fits the bulk but runs above all 13 high waters where the harbor reached 8.3 ft or more (by 0.18 ft on average), its residuals 0.10 ft against 0.03 for a transfer that bends near the marsh platform (4.29 ft NAVD88); the bending transfer floods the road at 8.75 ft; Oyster Creek (OYS-CR-25) bends the same way and floods only at 9.11 ft.
7. Paired River Road's campaign (HEN-RR-25) with the harbor and the USGS daily flows: the offset falls as the river falls (the staff checks rule out drift), so the transfer carries the flow; 2025-26 gives 10 days and the single campaign offset 26.
8. Ran every low point through the same weather years, each with its own transfer and that year's river flows: Wharf Street at Market Lane 32.3; Front Street at Ferry Lane 26.3; River Road at Hensley Landing 15.9; Marsh Road at Pellam Creek 14.3; Oyster Creek Road 1.2; ranked them and counted the years the same two lead (30 of 36).

No other pair survives. Straight-line transfers fund Marsh Road and River Road (33.6 and 36.7 days), but Marsh Road's own high waters above the marsh and River Road's own offset against the river's flow refute both; the drain rim and the untransferred harbor fund Front Street and River Road; the count trend and a Poisson range ignore the planning sea level and the clustering of tidal days. Every alignment of the weather years, both flow forms the campaign fits and every bending form of the marsh transfer keep Wharf Street and Front Street on top.
