Assumptions and judgment calls.

The source carries no cause flag per container-day. I inferred quota-rejection delay from portal closure days matched on unload date x port x line and treated it as additive to a moving drayage baseline. Charter wording ("regardless of which lifecycle stage timer was running") supports causal rather than stage attribution; it does not specify the estimator. A flat 0.44-day allowance yields drayage 3,234.740 / depot 3,015.966 (both still REMEDIATE) and model +/-10% yields depot 3,254.393 / 2,834.817, so no reasonable split in this family changes the recommendation. Only the extreme choice of assigning every reject-day Dray timer wholly to depot would flip drayage to MONITOR (1,823.752) and depot to 5,526.638; I rejected it on the 1,689.648976-day non-reject June-August surge.
Absence of rejection intensity effect: I used closure-day indicator, not quota_closed_rejections counts, because within non-summer reject rows delay did not scale with count; this is an interpretive choice, not Charter text.
Approved plan multiplier_2027 values were treated as authoritative Annex C inputs rather than recomputing share ratios from rounded table percentages; the empty-depot difference is <=0.0002 days and immaterial.
Settled means milestone SETTLED; 190 VOID/test excluded per Charter. Unload/empty-return events after 31 Dec 2026 remain with their 2026 lifecycle because discharge date is 2026 and quarters are discharge-based.
TMS exceptions were never used as root cause; settlement never gates designation. Bunker/Maut excluded. Warehouse/inventory/SKU data were not attribution inputs.
2026 EUR is an indicative Schedule B revaluation, not a carrier receivable/payable; Charter prices only projected 2027 exposure.
Release gates, escalation triggers, monitoring pace and Q3 re-cut in deck slides 10/12 are labelled governance proposals, not Charter mandates. Rail de-designation at an effective factor below ~0.750740 is arithmetic sensitivity, not a Charter clause.
The milestone excess_cdays_total rounding gap (0.000094 days) was not forced to reconcile; stage fields govern.
Net: no assumption among the moderate choices changes the slate; the one extreme attribution choice would, and is documented as rejected rather than silently adopted.
