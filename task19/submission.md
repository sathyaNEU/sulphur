## Final Recommendation

**Certify Port Ardain, Ballinrea, Drumcastle and Tullaghvane as qualifying for the 2027/28 urban-services supplement, report Newbrook as too close to call (chance of 25,000 or more 0.22, so half rate under circular 2026/14 paragraph 3.2), and Fort Larrick, Carnmoyle, Rossmullan, Glenarra, Loughmeen, Ardvale and Fennabeg as not qualifying**, on estimated usual residents of 320,200 for Kilvarra (90 per cent interval 317,000 to 323,400). ## Critical Components

1. The matcher links 92.3 per cent of the survey's Kilvarran citizens and 72.3 per cent of others to their own district's list, but the clerical review shows it misses true links (recall 96.5 and 87.9 per cent): on the list are **94.9** and **85.1 per cent**.
2. The register and the survey miss the same young men: births and survival put men per woman above the estimate's, so men aged 18 to 29 are raised by **1.095** and 30 to 44 by **1.048** (each within 0.04).
3. Kilvarra's 90 per cent interval is **317,000 to 323,400** (plus or minus 1.0 per cent); resampling the survey blocks with the linkage correction and the raising factors held fixed gives plus or minus 0.4.
4. The rolled-forward estimate of 325,247 is **5,050** above the estimate; **7,140** emigrants since mid-2021 are still listed and missing from the accounts, against 603 arrivals the accounts left out.

## Step-by-Step

1. Placed every register entry in the district its postcode belongs to through the postcode directory (codes retired in 2024 included, keying differences removed), and the survey's people and the follow-up's entries by their block.
2. Linked the survey's people to the register on names, dates of birth, sex and postcodes, allowing other romanisations, nicknames and Irish forms, swapped day and month, defaulted dates of birth and name order swapped; one person per record. On the clerical review the matcher links 96.5 per cent of Kilvarran citizens who are on their own district's list and 87.9 per cent of others; the table of verified status against linked status, by citizenship and sex, gives each survey person their chance of being on their own district's list (94.9 and 85.1 per cent overall).
3. Measured each district's correct entries from the follow-up: resident at the address, or moved within the district; movers with no district recorded at the share the district's known movers give; people the household could not place and vacant addresses at the district's placed outcomes; no-contact entries at the chance a logistic model fitted on the resolved ones gives (last attendance, registration date, dwelling type, age, sex, district). Entries not correct: Tullaghvane 3,410, Carnmoyle 3,660, Newbrook 3,110.
4. Divided each district's correct entries, by age group and sex, by the share of the survey's people in that cell on the district's list (each district's own register count carrying its figure, not the sample blocks' entries expanded by their selection probabilities), serving personnel left out of both sides and students counted where the survey found them, at their term-time addresses (the enrolment return is not added on top, which would count them twice); the Defence Forces' return's 857 at Larrick Barracks were added to Fort Larrick, because paragraph 2.2 counts personnel at the base where they are stationed, and a serving member's civilian entry at another address is an entry that should not count there.
5. Built men per woman for Kilvarrans aged 18 to 29 and 30 to 44 from births by sex and the life table's survival, taking Kilvarrans' net migration since birth as the same for men and women, and compared it with the estimate's (serving personnel added back on that side): raising factors 1.095 and 1.048 applied to every district's men of those ages. Independence leaves Kilvarra 4,050 lower.
6. Resampled the survey's blocks within districts and repeated every step, the clerical review's table and the raising factors included: Kilvarra 320,200 (317,000 to 323,400); each district's chance of 25,000 or more from its estimate and spread, and the call under paragraph 3.2.
7. Bridged each register count to its estimate, the parts adding up, and the rolled-forward figure to the estimate: emigrants since mid-2021 still listed (the follow-up's people who left Kilvarra in July 2021 or later by the month the household gave, no leaving month falling after June 2026, the undated shared as the dated are and those not known at the address shared out as the district's known outcomes are) less those whose departure the border system recorded, which the accounts already hold: 7,140.

No other call survives. Independence lowers the districts by 1.1 to 1.6 per cent and on its own leaves Newbrook not qualifying; reading the matcher's links as coverage lifts them by 2.3 to 5.9 per cent and leaves Carnmoyle too close to call and Newbrook qualifying. Expanding the sample blocks by their selection probabilities instead of scaling each district's register count carries the sample's own sampling error into each figure: the expanded entries miss the known list by -3.1 to 3.3 per cent, which scaling to the list removes. Calibrating to the rolled-forward total carries the emigrants the accounts never recorded into every district (Kilvarra 325,250, the call unchanged only because Newbrook's interval straddles the line either way, while every district's figure carries the emigrants); reading the register's own counts carries them and the duplicates too, and puts Carnmoyle and Newbrook over the line.
