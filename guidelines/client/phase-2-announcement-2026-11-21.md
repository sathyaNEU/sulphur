@channel Welcome back to Project Sulfur!! The client love the first phase, so we get to do more tasks! So congrats
to you all! Tasks will be released in the next couple of hours. Here are the updates, please read this thoroughly
before tasking:

- You can find the overall guidelines here: https://project-sulfur.learn.joinhandshake.com/nov-09-updates
- There will be 2 rollouts instead of 1 now. The average is still <60%. Rollouts are much cheaper so we will be
  loosening the rules on excessive rollouts. However, you should still NOT be spamming.
- The depth-scale "deep reasoning" rules are still in place and we want to emphasize that we will not be accepting
  tasks that have shallow stumps:
  - Planted defect or decoy
  - Rule application
  - Shallow hidden factor
  - Data semantics
  - "Replay"
  - One thing deciding the answer

Most importantly, there shouldn't just be "one major step" that decides the entire problem. There should be at
least two independent and challenging steps that the model could reasonably get wrong and are not a banned stumping
method.

- Historically, a lot of tasks just came down to "spot this one thing" and you get the right answer. If you miss it,
  the answer completely changes. We are trying to avoid that.
- I think the best way to go about creating a problem is NOT "how can I plant a file or change some
  numbers/sentences to trick the model and see if it notices it".
- Instead, try to come up a challenging multi-step and multi-part analytical scenario that in your industry
  experience models have trouble with in data analytics

Tasks already submitted will be reviewed and must follow these rules (the spec hasn't really changed. it's basically
the same as last time, just adding more clarification)

- You can find the depth scale here: https://project-sulfur.learn.joinhandshake.com/depth-scale
- You can find real examples here. Do NOT just copy them, they are likely already overused stumps:
  https://project-sulfur.learn.joinhandshake.com/examples
- Do not template your own tasks or use these overused stumps:
  https://project-sulfur.learn.joinhandshake.com/overused-stumping-strategies. Please be creative. Data Analytics is
  such a big field, there are many avenues out there.
