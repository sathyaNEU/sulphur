# The Critical Components question on the submission form, read 2026-11-21

Kept verbatim as the author pasted it on 2026-11-21. It is the prompt of the form's Critical Components field, and it
asks what section 4 of `guidelines-2026-11-20.md` sets out (list two or more; the answer, not the method; each answer
gradable; independent). The `## Critical Components` block of `submission.md` is what is pasted there.

> What are the two or more decisive and challenging steps the model must get right to reach your final answer?
>
> Each step should be independently hard: a real deep-reasoning place where a strong model can go wrong, not a planted
> defect, a rule to follow, or one detail to spot.
>
> Missing one step should not sink the others. For each step, give the step and its correct answer (the figure, set,
> classification or finding it produces, e.g. "the restated 2023 baseline is $4.2M", not "restate the baseline").
