## What the two findings say about the build

- **The domain reads as a backdrop.** The call was a docket of fifteen under a stated threshold, and three of the
  four asks were a count of filers against a deadline and two tabulations of published and disclosed dollars. The
  one estimate in the task (each member's 2026 remuneration ahead of publication) sat under those, and the checker
  read the whole as rule application: "compliance lookups, counts, and certified figures under a stated rule". The
  subdomain's own work, as the finding names it: undercoverage or nonresponse bias in a disclosure registry,
  small-area or administrative-data methods for missing or misreported payment data.
- **The prompt carried the slices.** The tables' breakdowns, the year range and the two sources to compare were
  all in the request, which the checker counts as method spelled out, and the deliverables ran as a roll call that
  closed on a file. The backslashes came from the portal's own text box escaping the underscores in a file name: a
  prompt that names no file cannot carry them.
- **Under the bar of 2026-11-09 the build was already due a re-root** (`precut.py` marks task1 to task14): one
  deep step (each disclosure read to the day it was filed) decided the docket, and the asks around it were
  tabulations.
