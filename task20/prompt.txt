How much can Wexmoor University's endowment commit to new private funds in 2027 and still pass its liquidity policy, whose test rests on estimates of capital calls and distributions its funds have yet to make? Corrigan Hart's pacing plan puts the year at USD 500 million. Show the largest 2027 total that passes, in USD million, and at USD 500 million the policy's net cash needs over 2027 and 2028 and the headroom left above the minimum reserve, both in USD million. For the commitments made by the end of 2026, give the capital calls in the policy's stress in each half of 2027 and 2028 in USD million, split between funds that have held a first close and funds yet to hold one, the distributions in the same stress in each half in USD million, and the commitments not yet called at 31 December 2026 by strategy in USD million. A workbook and a one-page PDF memo.
