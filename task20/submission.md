# task20: Wexmoor University's endowment, the 2027 private-fund program

## Tags

**Domain:** Quantitative Finance, Liquidity, funding & balance-sheet risk (an endowment's private-fund commitment program under its liquidity policy: capital calls in a stressed exit and credit market).
**Analytical objective:** Forecasting & Predictive Modeling.

## Final Recommendation

Adopt program B (Oakhanger Capital V, Wendlebury Growth VI, Corvane Ventures VI and Ewelme Real Estate VI, USD 365 million) and defer Harpsden Credit V to 2028: before any 2027 commitment the policy's stress leaves USD 214.3 million above the USD 1,535 million minimum, enough for program B's USD 180.1 million of calls but not for program A's USD 288.9 million. Not program A, which the careful reading adopts by holding every investment period to its registered end and the real-asset loans as they stand (USD 305.7 million of headroom), nor program C, which a reading that refinances the loans but misses the successor funds' first closes adopts (USD 167.5 million).

No other conclusion survives the records. The two judgments pull the headroom in opposite directions, so each alone lands on a wrong program: the successor closes take calls out and the refinancing gaps put more in, and only both together leave program B passing and program A failing.
