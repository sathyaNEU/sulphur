# task20: Wexmoor University's endowment, the 2027 private-fund program

## Tags

**Domain:** Quantitative Finance, Liquidity, funding & balance-sheet risk (an endowment's private-fund commitment program under its liquidity policy: capital calls in a stressed exit market).
**Analytical objective:** Forecasting & Predictive Modeling.

## Final Recommendation

Adopt program D, the venture re-up only (Corvane Ventures VI, USD 45 million), and defer the other re-ups to 2028: before any 2027 commitment the policy's stress leaves USD 78.3 million above the USD 1,150 million minimum, enough for D's USD 18.4 million of calls but not for program C's USD 123.8 million, B's USD 169.3 million or A's USD 247.6 million. Not the careful reading that holds the support curve flat past the shortfall the endowment's record reaches and carries the six managers' last audited borrowings (program A), nor the textbook pacing model on the capital accounts (program A).
