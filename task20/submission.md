# task20: Hallingvass Kraft's committed margin facility for 2027

## Final Recommendation

Commit EUR 350 million for 2027 (EUR 150 million is committed for 2026): in a year whose prices and inflows repeat 2022, the hedge program's cumulative net cash outflow peaks at EUR 328.8 million on 27 August 2027 (any figure from EUR 320 million to EUR 345 million and from 23 to 31 August 2027 is within method), which the standard rounds up to EUR 350 million. Reject the EUR 300 million the careful but hurried route reaches (the book grown along the policy's minimum on exchange positions, 2022's own margin rates and output carried over, the affiliate paid at spot) and the EUR 450 million a hedge ratio counted on exchange positions alone gives.

## Critical Components

1. The hedge ratio counts the fixed-price Fjordal Aluminium contract (100 MW at EUR 38.00/MWh to 2029) as the policy's "sold forward at a fixed price" does: at 31 December 2026 the ratios are 68.6, 39.6 and 22.2 per cent, against 46.5, 17.4 and 0.0 per cent on the exchange book alone, and only this basis reproduces every trade the desk made since 2018; counted on exchange positions the desk would sell enough more to push the requirement to EUR 438.5 million.
2. The desk steps each delivery year to the policy maximum, 3.0 points a week, once its year contract trades more than 1.25 times its October budget price, and sells nothing above the minimum otherwise (this rule reproduces the trade capture exactly); in the stress year that puts 2.222 TWh of 2028, 1.077 TWh of 2029 and 0.964 TWh of 2030 on the exchange at the peak, against a requirement of EUR 227.4 million if the book only follows the minimum.
3. NEX's rate for each class is the front contract's settlement price times the 99th percentile of its absolute two-day log moves over the last 250 business days, reviewed on Thursdays and changed when the indicated rate is more than 10 per cent from the rate in force; replayed on 2027 this gives initial margin of EUR 59.7 million at the peak, where 2022's own rates as a share of price give EUR 117.2 million (2022's look-back still held December 2021) and rates held at the year end give a requirement of EUR 284.1 million.
4. Storage opens 2027 at 875 GWh against a licence floor of 700 GWh: the plants' release falls in a straight line with storage below normal and stops at the floor, so 2022's dry spring holds output to 1,743 GWh by the peak and the supply contracts' shortfall is bought day-ahead (EUR 82.3 million net); 2022's own shortfall carried over, or output scaled with each week's inflow, gives EUR 282.8 million or EUR 277.4 million.
5. Hallingvass Straum pays the average system price of the 12 months ending 2 months before delivery (every invoice since 2019 to the cent), so customer payments to the peak are EUR 173.7 million; paid at spot like the other customers, the requirement falls to EUR 265.9 million.

## Step-by-Step

1. Rebuild the book: the position statement of 30 December 2026 gives 1.848 TWh of 2027 and 0.685 TWh of 2028 on the exchange; the trade capture agrees once cancelled trades are dropped, amended prices kept and the MWh-entered trades converted.
2. Read the hedge policy against the books: on exchange positions alone the next delivery year sat more than five points below the policy minimum at 64 of the 84 month ends since 2020, a gap the Fjordal contract's fixed-price volume closes; counted with it, the ratios at the year end are 68.6, 39.6 and 22.2 per cent. A hurried pass applies the ladder to exchange positions and sells too much.
3. Recover the desk's rule from the trade capture against the October budget prices: it sells only to the minimum until a year contract trades 1.25 times budget, then 3.0 points a week to the maximum. The policy's minimum ladder (EUR 227.4 million) and selling to the maximum at once both miss it.
4. Recover NEX's rule from the margin notices and the front contracts' settlement prices (that look-back, percentile, review day and tolerance reproduce 212 of the 222 notices since 2019 to the cent) and run it through the stress year from the 2026 look-back. 2022's rates as a share of price (EUR 386.2 million) carry December 2021 in their window; rates held at the year end (EUR 284.1 million) or scaled with the front price miss the stress altogether.
5. Build the stress year as the standard says: each forward month moved by 2022's percentage on the same date, the system price at 2022's ratio to the front month, each week's inflow at 2022's ratio to normal.
6. Run the plants from the year-end storage: run-of-river takes 26 per cent of inflow up to 60 GWh a week, the reservoirs release their normal plan scaled down in a straight line with storage below normal and never below the licence floor; output to the peak is 1,743 GWh, and the shortfall against committed supply is bought day-ahead and settled the next business day.
7. Price the customers as the ledger shows: Fjordal at EUR 38.00, Nesbyen and Bygdenett at the month's average system price, Hallingvass Straum on its twelve-month lagged average; each pays on its own habit (39, 30, 51 and 21 days after the invoice).
8. Add the day's cash: variation margin and delivery settlement against NEX's settlement prices, the change in initial margin at the replayed rates, day-ahead net purchases, less customer payments. The cumulative sum peaks at EUR 328.8 million on 27 August 2027; the standard sets EUR 350 million.
9. Check the method on 2022 itself: from the end-2021 book it gives EUR 548.2 million on 26 August 2022, against EUR 548.5 million on 26 August 2022 in the records. The check cannot tell the hurried route from the right one, since 2022's own rates and output reproduce 2022 by construction; each of the five steps is fixed by its own record instead.
10. No other size survives: every defensible variant (the desk's threshold and pace, the rule's quantile convention and look-back, the release response, the payment habits and the affiliate's window) lands between EUR 320 million and EUR 345 million and sets EUR 350 million, while each hurried step alone moves the requirement by 10 to 37 per cent and the careful but hurried pass lands at EUR 297.1 million (a EUR 300 million facility).
