## Step-by-Step

1. The delivered panel, every issuer and merchant id, grew -8.90% on the same quarter a year earlier, mostly KS-06, whose last delivered week began 26 October 2025, and the latest weeks still posting.
2. Each issuer's first-delivered share of a week's final spend at lags 0 to 2 (medians over the 27 deliveries) completes the latest weeks (+1.21 points); the Hollister's shop-in-shops and the cafes, the licensees' sales, come out of the brand id by month (-0.57 points); issuers in both quarters (KS-01 to KS-05) with each card-open year set against the open year one later give like-for-like growth of 19.48% (+27.74 points).
3. The filing's fiscal 2024 net sales by region weight each region's growth: 15.00% (-4.48 points); the private-label share carried at fiscal 2025's pace to 17.00%, with the 10-K's 12% of other tenders, turns bank-card spend into customer payments: 9.17% (-5.83 points).
4. The hurried analysis stops here and files payments as net sales: USD 1,581.4 million, range USD 1,551.5 to 1,611.3 million, a beat, with a record (root mean square error 1.61 points) that looks sound because no past December held an event this large and no past base held a contract this large.
5. Customers pay when they order and net sales are recognised on delivery, so retail net sales are payments less the change in customer deposits. Last year's closing deposits (USD 205.5 million), scaled by retail payments in the quarter's last eight weeks and by the made-to-order share of the banner's spend over them (38.90% against 29.01%), give USD 318.9 million at the quarter's end against USD 210.6 million in October: retail net sales USD 1,421.8 million (1.84%, -7.33 points).
6. Wholesale, invoiced and never on a card, was USD 52.5 million a year earlier and runs at USD 17.7 million (the median of the last eight quarters): net sales USD 1,439.5 million, -0.63% (-2.47 points).
7. The same chain at each of the 15 quarters from fiscal 2022 Q1 to fiscal 2025 Q3, on what was known then: root mean square error 1.12 points, 13 inside; the range is the estimate plus and minus 1.44 points, USD 1,418.6 to 1,460.3 million, below the street's USD 1,484.0 million.
