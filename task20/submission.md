# task20: Wexmoor University's endowment, the 2027 private-fund commitment budget

## Tags

**Domain:** Quantitative Finance, Liquidity, funding & balance-sheet risk (an endowment's private-fund commitment pacing under its liquidity policy: capital calls, distributions and first closes in a stressed exit market).
**Analytical objective:** Forecasting & Predictive Modeling.

## Final Recommendation

Approve a 2027 private-fund budget of no more than USD 250 million (USD 268.6 million before the policy's USD 25 million steps; band USD 175 million to USD 350 million), not Corrigan Hart's USD 500 million, which fails the liquidity policy by USD 123.3 million. Not the careful reading that takes the gap between the managers' investments and the capital called as recycled proceeds, gives every follow-on one pace and closes the pending funds on their managers' dates (USD 850 million), nor the textbook pacing model on the capital accounts (USD 550 million).

## Critical Components

1. The test at USD 500 million (synthesis): the reserve of **USD 2,137.4 million** less net cash needs of **USD 1,060.7 million** leaves USD 1,076.7 million, **USD 123.3 million below** the USD 1,200 million minimum; a USD 250 million budget leaves **USD 9.9 million** above it.
2. The calls of the funds that have held a first close (decomposition and modeling): **USD 517.0 million** in the first half of 2027, of which **USD 237.7 million** is capital the managers had invested on their funds' subscription lines at 31 December and call at the clean-down; defensive follow-ons add **USD 212.8 million** over the eight quarters (USD 181.6 to 247.7 million across the defensible fits).
3. The calls of the funds yet to close (forecasting): **nothing in 2027**, **USD 63.7 million** and **USD 85.2 million** in the two halves of 2028, the four funds' first closes slipping 2.5 and 3.8 quarters.

## Step-by-Step

1. Assemble the commitments: the capital account statements less the superseded row, the custodian's fourth-quarter cash for the funds whose statement has not arrived, the commitments released at a fund's term end left out and the four funds yet to close counted in full: USD 4,191.1 million not yet called at 31 December 2026.
2. The reserve and the spending: the year-end holdings at the policy's factors, USD 2,137.4 million; eight quarters of a quarter of FY2027's approved USD 286.5 million distribution, USD 573.0 million.
3. Distributions as the policy sets them: each fund's 31 December value (the late statements' 30 September value plus the fourth quarter's calls less its distributions) times its strategy's 2008Q3 to 2010Q2 benchmark rate, the benchmark funds' distributions over their previous-quarter value: H1 2027 402.8; H2 2027 280.2; H1 2028 169.5; H2 2028 198.4.
4. The capital invested and not yet called: for each fund, the endowment's share of the investments in the managers' activity less the investment capital it was called for; at each audited year-end the part not on the fund's credit facility (borrowings at the endowment's share) is proceeds recycled, carried to the end of 2026 at the manager's own recycling rate: USD 237.7 million on the lines at 31 December. The 2022 and 2023 calls show each manager cleaning its lines down once the line's all-in cost passed its own limit; the stress's 5.25 per cent short rate passes every manager's limit, so the stock is called in the first quarter of 2027. Reading the whole gap as recycled proceeds leaves it out and gives USD 700 million.
5. New deals and planned follow-ons: each strategy's pace on the room left by year since first close, the deal market's elasticity on the exit index 0.04; fees on the commitment while investing and on the cost held afterwards; recycling at each manager's rate.
6. Defensive follow-ons: follow-ons into companies held two years or more, as a share of those companies' cost each quarter, rise once the exit index two quarters earlier sits below 100 (0.64 per cent of the cost a quarter for each 10 points below, or about half that on the companies' latest value, which carries the same calls; the threshold and the lag chosen by fit on 2020 and 2022 to 2024). On the stress path they add USD 212.8 million while new deals slow. One investment pace for every follow-on misses the rise and gives USD 600 million.
7. First closes: on the register's closes, a fund's first close slips 0.61 quarters for each 10 points the exit index sat below 100 in the quarter before (window and reference chosen by fit). On the stress path the three funds due in the third quarter of 2027 slip 2.5 quarters and the one due in the fourth 3.8, so the four close in 2028: USD 148.9 million of calls in the eight quarters, not the USD 256.1 million their managers' dates give. On those dates the budget is USD 50 million.
8. The budget: spread across the pacing plan's funds in its proportions, each USD 100 million calls USD 53.3 million over the eight quarters; at USD 500 million the net cash needs are USD 1,060.7 million and the headroom USD -123.3 million; the largest budget that passes is USD 268.6 million, USD 250 million in the policy's steps.
9. The shortcuts land elsewhere: the careful route USD 850 million; the pacing model on the capital accounts USD 550 million, recalibrated on 2022 and 2023 USD 150 million; the benchmark's 2008-10 call rates replayed USD 50 million; the latest audited borrowings carried as the line stock USD 625 million; each of the three judgments alone on the careful route USD 400, 500 and 1,050 million.

No other conclusion survives the records. The calls of 2027 and 2028 are the managers' own: capital they have already invested on credit and will call when the stress makes the credit dear, defensive money into the companies they cannot sell, and new deals that slow; the pending funds call only once they close, and the register shows closes slipping when exits are weak. Each of the three moves the budget outside the band on its own, in different directions, and every rival fit, the bootstrap's spread over funds and the next-best windows keep it between USD 175 and USD 350 million, well short of USD 500 million.
