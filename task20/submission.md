# task20: Wexmoor University's endowment, the 2027 private-fund program

## Tags

**Domain:** Quantitative Finance, Liquidity, funding & balance-sheet risk (an endowment's private-fund commitment program under its liquidity policy: capital calls in a stressed exit and credit market).
**Analytical objective:** Forecasting & Predictive Modeling.

## Final Recommendation

Adopt program B (Oakhanger Capital V, Wendlebury Growth VI and Corvane Ventures VI, USD 245 million) and defer Ewelme Real Estate VI and Harpsden Credit V to 2028: before any 2027 commitment the policy's stress leaves USD 209.7 million above the USD 1,170 million minimum, enough for program B's USD 119.2 million of calls but not for program A's USD 291.7 million. Not program A, which the careful reading adopts by holding every investment period to its registered end and the real-asset loans as they stand (USD 326.7 million of headroom), nor program C, which a reading that refinances the loans but misses the successor funds' first closes adopts (USD 78.1 million).

## Step-by-Step

1. Assemble the commitments: the statements less the superseded row, the custodian's fourth-quarter cash for the late statements, released commitments out, the pending funds in full: USD 4,506.4 million not yet called.
2. The reserve (USD 2,778.7 million at the policy's factors), the spending (eight quarters of a quarter of FY2027's distribution, USD 791.0 million) and the distributions at the policy's 2008-10 rates: H1 2027 603.6; H2 2027 443.4; H1 2028 237.9; H2 2028 265.8.
3. The capital invested through the funds' credit facilities and not yet called, formed as policy section 8 sets out (each fund's latest audited borrowings at Wexmoor's share, plus its investments since on the managers' activity file and less the capital called for investments since, never below nil): USD 316.1 million, counted in the first quarter. The latest audited borrowings alone show USD 49.2 million; the facilities were drawn again through 2026.
4. What section 8 sets, applied as written. New investments while a fund's investment period runs, the quarter it ends included: the pace of its strategy and year since first close (first and follow-on investments on the managers' activity file over the commitment not yet invested, at Wexmoor's share) on its own commitment not yet invested, USD 1,134.8 million over the eight quarters. No period is extended in the stress, section 8 making no allowance for the exit market. Fees and expenses at each fund's latest statement (none on top of the benchmark's rate for a buyout, growth or venture fund past its period), and at its strategy's median quarter for a fund yet to close, USD 174.4 million. Funds yet to hold a first close on their managers' dates. Buyout, growth and venture funds past their investment periods (32 at 31 December, worth USD 2,170.7 million, and those whose periods end inside the eight quarters from the quarter after): the 31 December value times the benchmark's call rate of 2008Q3 to 2010Q2 for funds with a vintage six or more years before the quarter's year (12.13, 12.28 and 9.02 percent of value over the eight quarters for buyout, growth and venture), USD 278.4 million once no fund calls more than its commitment has left. That cap runs quarter by quarter: each quarter's calls come off the commitment the fund has left, and within a quarter they are counted in this order until it is used up: the facility balance, fees and expenses, the benchmark's rate, new investments, and last a maturing loan's shortfall (taking the shortfall first leaves the headroom where it is). A fund that has used its commitment up calls nothing more, its fees included, so the fees and expenses above are USD 180.8 million before the cap. A fund whose term ends inside the eight quarters calls nothing after it.
5. The successor closes: the register records, for every past fund whose successor closed before its agreed investment period was out, whether the period ended at the successor's first close (61 such successions). 16 managers' funds always stopped there (the clause of their agreements) and 8 managers' ran on (Bix Direct Lending, Harpsden Credit, Kelstone Equity, Lewknor Credit Partners, Nettlebed Property Partners, Sallowfield Capital, Shabbington Infrastructure, Waterperry Infrastructure). For the managers with the clause, a fund's investment period ends the quarter before its successor's first close, on the manager's date as section 8 takes it: Oakhanger Capital IV (successor Oakhanger Capital V, Q1 2027); Wendlebury Growth V (successor Wendlebury Growth VI, Q1 2027); Ewelme Real Estate V (successor Ewelme Real Estate VI, Q1 2027); Corvane Ventures V (successor Corvane Ventures VI, Q1 2027); Thornwick Capital V (successor Thornwick Capital VI, Q3 2027); Rudgely Equity VI (successor Rudgely Equity VII, Q3 2027); Brackley Growth VII (successor Brackley Growth VIII, Q4 2027). From then the fund stops investing, and a buyout, growth or venture fund moves to the benchmark's rate. This is where the hurried forecast goes wrong first: it holds every period to the end the register records, and calls USD 131.6 million more.
6. The refinancing calls: the activity file's asset-loan records give each property and infrastructure asset's loan and maturity, and 35 past refinancings, 5 of them at a lower balance with the fund investing the difference. The lenders' limit, fitted on the 26 refinancings since mid-2022 (the new balance over the asset's value, which is the fund's valuation of its holding the quarter before plus the loan replaced), is the lower of 0.690 of value and 3.720 over the rate plus 2.25 points (0.496 of value at the stressed rate of 5.25 percent). The assets' values (valuation plus loan) move with equities (an elasticity of 0.70) and with the short rate (0.035 per point), both a quarter late, fitted on the 743 quarterly changes in value between two valuations both sixteen quarters or more after the asset's loan was first drawn (its four-year plan behind it), in quarters the loan did not change; the lag is the record's own, the same fit with no lag or two quarters late leaving the changes largely unexplained. So a loan maturing in a quarter is refinanced on the asset's 31 December value moved by equities and the short rate from 30 September 2026 to the end of the quarter before. Of the loans alive at 31 December, 23 fall due in the eight quarters (USD 839.6 million at Wexmoor's share); at the stressed rate and values the lenders grant USD 279.5 million less than the balances, and the funds call USD 254.9 million of it within their commitments (H1 2027 46.7; H2 2027 137.2; H1 2028 38.1; H2 2028 32.9). This is the second place the hurried forecast goes wrong: it carries the loans as they stand.
7. The test: the headroom before any 2027 commitment is USD 209.7 million; each re-up's calls over the eight quarters (Oakhanger Capital V 60.4; Wendlebury Growth VI 41.1; Corvane Ventures VI 17.7; Ewelme Real Estate VI 63.1; Harpsden Credit V 109.4) give program A USD 291.7 million, B USD 119.2 million, C USD 58.8 million and D USD 17.7 million; the largest program that passes is B, with USD 90.5 million to spare, and program A falls USD 82.0 million short.
8. The shortcuts land elsewhere: the careful route (periods to their registered ends, loans as they stand) on program A; the successor closes found but the loans carried on program A; the loans refinanced but the periods held on program C; the loans refinanced at 31 December's terms on program A, and at the stressed rate on 31 December's values on program A; section 8's benchmark rate carried over to the property, infrastructure and credit funds on program A; the pacing model on the capital accounts on program A as it stands and on program A recalibrated on the 2022 to 2024 exit drought; the benchmark's 2008-10 call rates replayed on the commitments not yet called on program A.

No other conclusion survives the records. The two judgments pull the headroom in opposite directions, so each alone lands on a wrong program: the successor closes take calls out and the refinancing shortfalls put more in, and only both together leave program B passing and program A failing. What the policy leaves to the evidence moves the headroom only between USD 195.5 and 215.4 million across every rival fit (the lenders' limit, the assets' values on every valuation or on the settled ones, the order a fund's calls meet its commitment, investment periods extended as past weak exit markets extended them), against USD 90.5 million of room below and USD 82.0 million above. The bases section 8 files are not close calls either, each reading its sentences rule out leaving program B: the benchmark's funds a year younger or older than it says (USD 167.1 or 248.5 million of headroom), the pace over the commitment not yet called (USD 224.1 million), the facility balances as the investments less the capital called for them with the audited borrowings set aside (USD 166.0 million), and the funds' own fees on top of the benchmark's rate (USD 141.0 million).
