Assumptions and judgment calls.

Peak 15-minute active demand is estimated as the coincident peak-hour final SCADA observation because no pool sub has a 15-minute load series. This is the most consequential assumption. A different reasonable choice cannot be derived from supplied pool records: PQ is excluded modernized stations and monthly registers are noncoincident. It does not merely relabel data, so the deliverables label it as an estimate and Method rows 9, 16, 20 and 27 disclose it. If a future verified uniform peak factor exceeded Kvernholm 1.0337 or Malmnes 1.1112, the schedule would change; I recommend Commissioning a January 2027 15-minute PQ campaign and lateral phase survey for those two before order placement. Adopting PQ/register uplift now would be unjustified and could flip Kvernholm/Malmnes.
Peak-hour net Q uses fixed January capacitor compensation inferred from settled meters. Reasonable alternatives: use manual load Q only (changes Kvernholm timing and sizes), or January monthly net Q/P ratio (tiers unchanged but relief 22.216 not 24.930). I chose fixed-bank peak-hour subtraction because Section 5.1 asks for peak-hour Q and a transformer carries net bus Q; monthly ratio averages off-peak hours. If the utility instead defines filing Q as meter-equivalent monthly ratio, relief sizing would be 22.216 MVA but the assigned timing schedule would remain as recommended.
Legacy lateral peaks are unmeasured; I scale GIS annual billed energy by AMI weighted class peak ratio at the actual peak hour. Unweighted peer ratios change results by <= 0.034 percentage points; AMI-only is not a permissible interpretation because Section 3.2 requires all single-phase laterals and it erases Svarthaug's C-phase legacy concentration (legacy phase C annual energy 25,537.0 MWh vs A 6,821.6 and B 7,747.7), changing its tier. A peer-matching or phase-specific ratio could move Svarthaug/Kvernholm relief somewhat but not Svarthaug's Phase 1 (its threshold breach is 25.580 percentage points) and not Kvernholm's Phase 2 under net-Q method (3.446-point headroom to Phase 1, 11.554 above Phase 2 floor).
GIS phases accepted for in-scope because the commissioning log covers only excluded modernized stations; verification years were not used to reject records. If GIS is stale for pool sites, limiting-phase identity and relief could change materially, especially Svarthaug C; no supplied in-scope trace overrides it.
Raudfjord clock treated as fixed CET; January peak unaffected. DST offsets trusted. Final batch preferred over preliminary.
Modernized stations excluded even though PQ data exist; relief pool per Section 2. Tiers unrounded; inclusive lower bounds.
Peak hour specified per site even though standard asks 15-minute; phase shares therefore estimated at peak hour, not at an unknown 15-minute subinterval.
Alternatives rejected.

Treat demand-register January maxima as coincident peak / sum them: rejected because sums exceed SCADA peaks at every site (Drangvik 51.807 vs 31.050; Malmnes 56.676 vs 30.573; Tverrli 32.993 vs 20.231 MW) and the modernized PQ cross-check proves noncoincidence (Fossheim October registers 40.032 vs PQ max 28.489 MW; Lindberg 41.277 vs 36.579). Using them would inflate P by tens of percent and falsely tier most Defer sites.
Use PQ 15-minute data to uplift pool peaks: rejected because PQ covers only excluded Fossheim/Lindberg, factors are station- and hour-variable (Fossheim 1.008-1.327, Lindberg 1.005-1.758), and no rule transfers them to pool sites. It would arbitrarily flip Kvernholm at factor > 1.0337 and Malmnes at > 1.1112 without evidence. PQ also confirms SCADA is hourly average, motivating disclosure rather than silent 15-minute claim.
Use uncompensated engineering-manual load Q: rejected as filing Q because settled meters show large net reactive differences consistent with seasonal fixed compensation; this alternative would place Kvernholm at 105.131% Phase 1 instead of Phase 2 (and changes pool sizing), which is why it is explicitly sensitivity-tested, not hidden.
Use January monthly net Q/P ratio instead of peak-hour fixed cap: viable and tiers identical, but rejected for sizing because it averages low-load hours; pool relief 22.216 MVA vs adopted 24.930. Mentioned so reviewer can reproduce alternative.
AMI-only phase split: rejected against Section 3.2; makes Svarthaug 84.381% Defer versus 125.580% Phase 1 when required legacy C-phase laterals are included, directly reversing the largest relief item.
Use phase-bus voltage imbalance to infer phase load: rejected; no phase angle/current, correlations/signs vary by site (Kvernholm negative, Raudfjord positive, Svarthaug near zero), cannot reproduce lateral load.
Let modernization condition, DGA, tariff, streetlighting, 2025 energy, network voltages or LTC alter schedule: no NS-2026 input; Section 1.2 bars portfolio/condition overrides.
