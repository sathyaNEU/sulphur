## Deep reasoning

Task type: distribution-losses-energy-balance in Energy Systems & Utilities, which serves Data Extraction & Conformation (ETL / Pipeline Build): conforming seven regional 66/22 kV substations' 2026 peak 15-minute limiting-phase apparent loads from hourly SCADA, monthly feeder 15-minute demand registers, monthly revenue meters, transformer impedance records, GIS lateral connectivity, and hourly AMI lateral voltages.
Domain test: every decisive quantity is a three-phase electric distribution substation engineering calculation: correlated quadrature aggregation of non-coincident feeder 15-minute surges onto 60-minute SCADA energy blocks, inversion of peak-hour reactive power from HV/LV bus voltage drop across transformer nameplate resistance and reactance with LTC tap steps demeaned against monthly net kVARh to cancel PT calibration bias, and physical single-phase lateral phase identification via common-mode-rejected voltage differentials evaluated at the peak hour.
