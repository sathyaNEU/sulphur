- **Pool Total Relief Capacity Needed:** **50.51 MVA** (21-variant band 48.3 to 52.1 MVA).

Lindberg (110.1% measured vs 106.2% estimated) and Ostervik (82.4% measured vs 83.1% estimated) were modernized in 2023-2024 and are excluded from the 2027-2029 relief pool per NS-2026 Section 2. Every single one of the 21 honest modeling variants and all six +/-20% perturbations on fitted inputs file this exact seven-substation schedule. By contrast, the reflex shortcut (60-minute SCADA peak, monthly net kVARh power factor, and GIS phase tags) misplaces five substations and puts total relief at 19.50 MVA, while the rigorous standard shortcut (hourly coincidence times feeder 15-min registers, class nominal power factors, and GIS phase tags) misplaces all five open substations and puts total relief at 64.88 MVA.

## Critical Components

1. **Component 1 (15-Minute Coincident Active Peak Conformation):** Pool combined 2026 peak 15-minute active demand is **229.71 MW** (band 228.4 to 231.0 MW), reconstructed by correlated quadrature aggregation of monthly non-coincident feeder 15-minute excesses calibrated on Lindberg and Ostervik (rho_park = 0.7101, rho_indep = 0.0447, k_ord = 1.0459). Leaving this step out alone drops combined active demand to 208.24 MW and flips Drangvik from Phase 2 (94.5%) to Defer (83.0%).
2. **Component 2 (Peak-Hour Reactive Power Inversion with PT Demeaning):** Pool combined 2026 peak-hour reactive demand is **60.72 MVAr** (band 60.0 to 63.9 MVAr), inverted from hourly HV/LV bus voltage drop across transformer nameplate resistance and reactance with LTC tap steps (1.25% per step) and demeaned against monthly net kVARh to remove each substation's PT offset. Leaving this step out alone drops combined reactive demand to -14.22 MVAr and flips Kvernholm from Phase 1 (108.6%) to Phase 2 (92.4%).
3. **Component 3 (Physical Lateral Phase Identification):** Pool average peak limiting-phase share is **37.69%** (band 37.67% to 37.77%), recovered by regressing common-mode-rejected hourly AMI lateral voltage differentials on three-phase substation bus voltage differentials controlling for local lateral load drop (matching 72 of 72 field-traced laterals on Lindberg and Ostervik vs GIS 54 of 72, 75.0%). Leaving this step out alone drops the pool average to 36.33%, flips Raudfjord from Phase 1 (110.6%) to Phase 2 (87.0%), and flips Svarthaug from Phase 2 (92.7%) to Phase 1 (123.2%).

## Step-by-Step

1. **Conform Multi-System Operational Extracts (R1):** Load `scada_substation_hourly_2026.csv` (82,680 raw rows), deduplicate superseded preliminary batches (`extract_ts` latest wins, dropping 3,840 preliminary rows to obtain 78,840 clean substation-hours across 9 substations), align both Europe/Oslo CET/CEST ISO-8601 timestamps and Raudfjord's legacy `DD.MM.YYYY HH:MM` CET standard stamps to the exact 8,760-hour calendar-year 2026 Europe/Oslo grid, and linearly interpolate 48 benign off-peak summer comms nulls in `v_lv_kv`. Map legacy DMS feeder IDs across `feeder_scada_hourly_2026.csv` (converting kW to MW) and `feeder_demand_registers_monthly_2026.csv` using `feeder_id_crosswalk.csv` (42 feeders total). Load monthly revenue kWh and net kVARh from `revenue_meter_monthly_2026.xlsx`, transformer ratings and impedances from `substation_transformer_register.xlsx`, hourly 3-phase LV bus voltages from `substation_phase_bus_voltages_2026.csv`, GIS tags from `gis_lateral_connectivity_2026.csv` (324 laterals), and heating-season hourly AMI voltages and loads from `ami_lateral_telemetry_2026.db`. Exclude unused maintenance, streetlighting, and tariff files. Clean annual 60-minute SCADA peaks across the seven target substations sum to 208.24 MW.
2. **Reconstruct 15-Minute Coincident Active Peaks via Correlated Quadrature (R2):** On modernized substations Lindberg and Ostervik (`pq_archive_15min_2026.csv`), compute intra-hour feeder deviations `dP_15 - P_60` during winter months (Jan, Feb, Nov, Dec) to estimate shared industrial-park feeder correlation `rho_park = 0.7101`, independent feeder correlation `rho_indep = 0.0447`, and intra-hour reactive slope `dQ/dP = 0.3000`. Calibrate the order-statistic scaling ratio `k_ord = 1.0459` as the MW-weighted ratio of observed substation 15-minute peak excess to raw correlated quadrature across Lindberg and Ostervik winter monthly and annual peaks. For each target substation, compute each feeder's winter-average relative 15-minute excess `((M15 - P60_m) / P60_m) * P60(h_peak)`, combine feeders in correlated quadrature using `industrial_park_zone` from `substation_transformer_register.xlsx`, and add `k_ord * raw_quad` to the annual 60-minute SCADA peak. Pool total = 229.71 MW (Drangvik 36.01, Hestmark 44.55, Kvernholm 31.08, Malmnes 33.58, Raudfjord 29.02, Svarthaug 29.87, Tverrli 25.60 MW).
3. **Invert Peak-Hour Reactive Demand from Transformer Voltage Drop and Net kVARh (R3):** For each substation and hour, compute tap-adjusted secondary voltage ratio `tau = (22/66) * (1 + 0.0125 * tap_step)`, voltage drop `dV = V_hv * tau - V_lv`, and uncalibrated hourly reactive power `Q_uncal = (22.0 * dV - R_ohm * P_60) / X_ohm`. Because inductive potential transformers carry individual calibration offsets, demean `Q_uncal` over the substation's peak month against the monthly mean net reactive power `net_kvarh / (1000 * hours_in_month)` from `revenue_meter_monthly_2026.xlsx`, eliminating PT offset algebraically while preserving diurnal reactive swings. Average demeaned `Q_hat` over the substation's top-5 active-load hours to obtain peak-hour reactive demand `Q_h`, and project to the 15-minute peak via `Q_15 = Q_h + dQ/dP * (P_15 - P_60)`. Pool combined `Q_h` = 60.72 MVAr (Kvernholm 19.19, Hestmark 13.53, Svarthaug 6.56, Tverrli 6.38, Raudfjord 6.34, Drangvik 6.24, Malmnes 2.47 MVAr).
4. **Identify Physical Single-Phase Lateral Connectivity and Peak Limiting-Phase Share (R4):** Convert 3-phase LV bus voltages from kV to 230 V phase-to-neutral scale, subtract the hourly 3-phase mean common-mode bus voltage from both bus phases and AMI lateral voltages, and take hourly first differences over winter months. For each lateral, regress differenced common-mode-rejected lateral voltage on each candidate bus phase differential (`A`, `B`, `C`) and differenced lateral active load `dP_kW` (requiring positive bus-voltage slope > 0.1), assigning the physical phase with minimum residual sum of squares. Sum identified single-phase lateral kW by phase plus one-third of balanced three-phase load (`sub_p60 * 1000 - sum(lat_kw)`) over the top-5 peak hours to obtain `w_max`. Pool mean = 37.69% (Raudfjord 44.54%, Tverrli 39.43%, Hestmark 37.36%, Kvernholm 36.42%, Drangvik 36.39%, Malmnes 35.65%, Svarthaug 34.05%).
5. **Validate Against Modernized Reference Substations (R5):** Test the end-to-end pipeline blind on Lindberg and Ostervik against their measured 15-minute PQ archives (`pq_archive_15min_2026.csv`) and 2025 field phase-tracer commissioning log (`commissioning_phase_log_2025.csv`). Physical phase-ID matches 72 of 72 laterals (100.0%) compared to GIS's 54 of 72 (75.0%). Mean absolute Firm N-1 loading error across Lindberg (106.15% vs 110.07%) and Ostervik (83.07% vs 82.35%) is **2.32 pct pts**, outperforming every shortcut by 3.8x to 13.0x: raw un-demeaned dV 8.92 (3.8x), flat 15m/60m ratio 9.87 (4.3x), net kVARh PF 10.52 (4.5x), rigorous standard route 11.73 (5.1x), GIS phase tags 13.16 (5.7x), 60-min SCADA 13.51 (5.8x), and direct route 30.08 (13.0x).
6. **Synthesize Apparent Peak Loading, Relief Capacity, Schedule, and Robustness Battery (R6 to R8):** Compute `S_15m = sqrt(P_15^2 + Q_15^2)`, `Lim_MVA = 3 * w_max * S_15m`, `Loading % = 100 * Lim_MVA / Firm_N1_MVA`, and `Relief_MVA = max(0, Lim_MVA - 0.85 * Firm_N1_MVA)` per NS-2026 Section 3, then assign tiers per Section 4 (`>= 100.0%` Phase 1, `>= 85.0%` Phase 2, `< 85.0%` Defer). Re-run across all 21 honest modeling variants (all-year/peak-month windows, additive excess scaling, median correlation and `k_ord`, monthly-only `k_ord`, constant `Q_15` / constant PF, winter/annual voltage-drop anchors, measured `V_lv` scaling, single-hour / top-50 regression peak Q, 6-month heating-season / level / soft-posterior / single-hour phase-ID, and multi-stump combinations) plus six +/-20% forcing perturbations: every variant and every perturbation files the identical 7-substation schedule, establishing the bands reported above.
