4. Assumptions and judgment calls
Timing split is my rule, not an RP-04 requirement: winter = season-critical fog/roost work; pre-summer = repairs by 31 May 2027 ahead of June-Aug faults. RP-04 does not specify a seasonal work-list calendar. Moving insulator replacements into winter would not change membership/fix status, only scheduling.
Location realism over false precision: estimates have ≈0.1-0.35 km two-terminal uncertainty and worse single-ended; pole/span reports therefore use repeat/patrol evidence where it exists, and may not resolve adjacent poles. Line 13’s estimated spread exceeds 400 m, so I report the 104-106 run indicated by repeated patrol evidence rather than claim each fault’s exact span. A different reasonable pole choice for Line 5 87/88 or Line 10 47-49 would not change the work-list decision.
Line 16 pole 42 is the hardest call: I treated progressive resistance decline, July-Aug timing, phases and lockout as insulator-failure evidence and did not let the 17 Jul 2025 patrol’s dead bird override them. Reading that patrol as conclusive avian evidence would move pole 42 from pre-summer insulator replacement to winter avian guards; it does not remove a repeat or change any other item. Pole 43’s chipped skirt is inspection context, not proof.
Line 9 pole 145 assignment rests on chipped-skirt condition and near-constant relay behavior beyond the solar tap; current ratio cannot locate beyond the tap. If the true repeat is poles 146/147 rather than 145, the fix remains replacing the failed string at the faulted pole, but the named pole could change.
Cause fallback: unsupported residual faults are other or unknown (76 rows), not invented vegetation/equipment. Salt classification used terminal/fog/patrol evidence; 13 low-resistance fog-located control-log UNK faults were moved to salt. Space/time/resistance never overrides patrol or lightning physics.
Fault minutes use physical supply restoration, not OMS restore: OMS lags switching 3-37 min. If RP-04 were read as “OMS restore is official,” 2026 would be 2,580,675.896 member-minutes instead of 1,502,950.740 and all-years 10,894,366.309 instead of 6,523,577.068; interruptions/work list are unchanged. Fractional minutes retained because RP-04 is silent on rounding.
Washing model is screening evidence, not design withstand; deposits assume wash reset to zero and test strings equal line posts. No ESDD reading post-dates the 23-26 Oct gale; Line 2 §§1/6 are model-sensitive (linear 0.05854/0.06104 vs exponential 0.12641/0.16037). If actual added gale deposit were <≈0.025-0.030, both would flip to not due.
