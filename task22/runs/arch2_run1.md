Assumptions and judgment calls.

Unauthorized use is estimated, not proven. The central balance adopts the screened demand deficits as conditional
unauthorized-use estimates. Vacancy, fuel switching, meter faults, and changed occupants remain possible explanations.
The account list is for investigation, not enforcement.

Elm Grove's exclusion is sensitive. Its central leak share is 0.938%, rising to 1.151% with the lower suspected-use
deduction, 1.193% when turnover-confounded deficits are not deducted, and 1.068% with gas temperature 1 F warmer. This
is the main qualification to the adopted list.

Temperature transport outside winter is unvalidated. There are no May-October gas-temperature observations. The
workbook includes alternative models and temperature scenarios. The four selected mains districts remain above the
threshold under the disclosed all-day +/-1 F and suspected-demand +/-20% scenarios.

Current inventory defines remaining meters. The November 2, 2026 register is the available basis for the 100-meter
rule. Historical meter location is assumed unchanged unless the supplied records establish otherwise.

Scheduled completion is a conditional forecast. Downtown has no own-district historical program, and 73 scheduled
jobs repeat unresolved Old Town work. The base forecast assumes ordinary access-matched success for those repeat jobs;
separate downside scenarios show the effect of failure or delay.

Airport temperature as gas temperature: rejected because its outdoor validation error is materially worse and its

Uniform daily allocation: rejected in favor of the better-validated weather allocation. Uniform allocation would
change Halvorsen's main-program classification.

All unexplained gas is leakage: rejected because temperature under-registration, large-customer gas, damage, own use,
and unauthorized-use estimates must be separated.

All consumption anomalies are confirmed theft: rejected. Removing every suspected-use deduction would add East
Industrial, Elm Grove, Lakeview, and North End to the mains list. That counterevidence is disclosed; the central
estimate remains the adopted calculation.

All 976 scheduled jobs finish before heating season: rejected because unresolved access cases and long indoor
completion delays are present in the historical record.

October 1 as the heating-season cutoff: rejected because the supplied plan explicitly specifies November 1.
