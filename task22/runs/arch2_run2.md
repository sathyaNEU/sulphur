Assumptions and judgment calls.

Unauthorized-use estimates are provisional. The central recommendation uses the modeled allowance, but fuel
switching, unrecorded occupancy changes or meter faults remain possible. Setting the allowance to zero adds East
Industrial, Elm Grove and Lakeview to mains eligibility. Inspection can therefore change the list.

Temperature relationships transfer beyond the observed winter. There are no summer logger observations, and indoor
calibration is limited. A -2 F structural stress removes Riverside from mains eligibility; +2 F adds Halvorsen. The
added-meter district list is unchanged under these stresses.

Weather-shaped calendar allocation is preferred to equal-day allocation. Heating demand varies within reading
intervals. Equal-day allocation adds Halvorsen to mains eligibility, which is disclosed rather than hidden.

Current register entries define meters remaining in service. Temporarily vacant premises remain included where the
meter is physically listed. Historical volumes use historical hardware.

Daily date labels are the available timing resolution. Exact AMR read times are unavailable, so no invented
adjustment is made for the tariff's 9 a.m. gas-day boundary.

603 is a conservative planning allowance. Giving unresolved repeat cases zero credit is not a claim that they cannot
succeed. The forecast assumes the published dates are feasible and Old Town performance transfers to Downtown and
commercial premises. It does not model shared crew disruptions or promise completion of the newly added scope.

Alternatives rejected.

Use published 2.42% LAUF as district leakage. Rejected because it uses city-gate receipts, bills issued and broader
own use. The reconstructed district residual is 0.718%, with the differences separated in Reconciliation.

Use airport air temperature as gas temperature. Rejected by the logger evidence and blocked-validation errors. Indoor
meters particularly require separate treatment.

Ignore missing readings or interpolate every outage. Rejected because blocked validation favors peer-based
reconstruction, especially for Riverside's longer outage.

Treat every consumption drop as theft. Rejected. Vacancy and turnover exclusions, rebate adjustments and unconfirmed
status are explicit. The strongest 16 whole-load or heating-only signatures are inspection priorities, not findings
of culpability.

Assume all 976 scheduled appointments finish before heat. Rejected by documented access failures and completion
delays, especially for indoor and late-season appointments.

Treat the central mains list as insensitive to assumptions. Rejected. The workbook and deck explicitly show the
unauthorized-use, temperature and allocation scenarios that can change eligibility.
