## Literal reader

Executing the shipped sentences word for word: policy 2.1 makes a channel's return the twelve-month net revenue of the installs a period's spend buys, per euro of that spend; 2.2 makes an install a new consumer account's first install, bought by at most one channel whether or not the partner can name it; 2.3 counts every charge the stores or the checkout made in the 365 days from the install, at its transaction time, net of refunds; 2.4 takes off VAT by storefront, the stores' 15 per cent and the checkout's 3 per cent; 2.5 files the two plans' prices and the seven-day trial; section 1 places the channels of the plan's growth sheet; section 3 cuts under 0.90, holds from 0.90 and scales from 1.20. The prompt asks for each channel's 2027 return at the plan's weekly spend; how many payments on the monthly plan the installs each channel bought in the year to 2 November 2025 made in their first 12 months, per 100 installs; and how many installs the plan's 2027 spend will buy with the 12-month net revenue they will bring.
