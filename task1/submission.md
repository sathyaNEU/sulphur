## Critical Components

1. Every line's expected 36-month net revenue per start (forecasting): the funded lines run **3.67 to 6.82** times their cost; the paid social lines come to SOC-BHR **EUR 129**, SOC-ELN **EUR 196**, SOC-GAZ **EUR 222**, SOC-KBO **EUR 113** and the cashback lines at the Rundschau and the Kreisblatt to **EUR 223** and **EUR 205**, all under 3.5 times their cost.
2. The newest starts' survival (missingness and measurement): **10.1 per cent** of the paid social starts of November 2025 to April 2026 and **13.5 per cent** of the cashback starts are still paying two years in (12.0 and 15.6 on their channels' older starts' survival); paid search's **27.3 per cent** is above its older starts' 25.5.
3. The full price at the end of the introduction (causal reasoning): the April 2027 list prices add **5.5 to 6.6 points** to the share leaving when an introductory price ends.
4. Returns by household (data discovery): the July to September 2023 starts brought **EUR 150** per start on paid social and **EUR 265** direct over 36 months counted by household (EUR 128 and EUR 249 on the customer number alone).

## Step-by-Step

1. From subscriptions_2023-01_2026-10.csv, price_periods_2023-01_2026-10.csv and customers.csv, drop the repeated export rows, the staff, gift and company products and the test accounts (the KPI standard), map the renamed channel codes, and count the subscriptions charged on 31 October 2026: 12,330 in all (Rundschau 5,112), where every exported row charged in October gives more.
2. Link households across customer numbers through payment_methods.csv (the payment fingerprint) and customers.csv (the normalised address), as the KPI standard counts households: the July to September 2023 starts brought EUR 150 per start on paid social over 36 months, not the EUR 128 on the customer number alone.
3. Fit the end of the introductory price on every step-up in the record by offer, channel and title with a term for the full price the start steps up to (offer_catalogue.csv, price_list_digital_2027.pdf), which the step-ups either side of the April 2025 rise identify: at 2026 prices the 2027 offers lose 67.0 (Probemonat) to 72.2 per cent (free quarter), and the April 2027 prices add 5.5 to 6.6 points, where a period term for the 2025 jump adds nothing.
4. Join reading_by_login_2024-01_2026-10.csv to logins.csv and sum each customer's logins; fit the full-price leaving rate on months since the step-up, channel and the first three months' reading, and complete the young starts on their own reading: 10.1 per cent of the paid social starts of November 2025 to April 2026 are still paying three years in, not the 12.0 their channel's older starts imply.
5. Read the April 2025 rise's extra cancellations from the cancellations received after the February 2025 notice letters through the second month at the new price, against each paid-tenure band's own rate, and carry them to each subscription's paid tenure at the April 2027 rise, which gives 420 extra cancellations by 31 December 2027 (the KPI standard's extra cancellations), not the 229 an after-the-date, pooled reading gives.
6. Carry the plan's 2,960 planned starts month by month through their introductory prices, the end of intro at the April 2027 list prices and the full-price leaving rate on their own reading: 2,004 still paying on 31 December 2027 (paid social 403 of 735), where the shortcuts above leave 2,083.
7. Value each line of media_plan_2027.xlsx by survival arithmetic over its planned start months, the reading and payment routes of its starts in the twelve months to October 2026 (as the KPI standard takes them), its 2027 offer at the April 2027 prices, the store fees and the household's first return through the paywall inside the 36 months: from EUR 372 per start (SEA-BHR) down to EUR 98 (TEL-ELN).
8. Fund the lines at 3.5 times their planned cost per start or more (the KPI standard): 8 lines for EUR 84,308; the shortcuts above together fund 14 lines for EUR 141,575.

No other conclusion survives the records. Completing the newest starts on their channel's older survival is ruled out by the reading log, where the starts of the twelve months to October 2026 read less (paid social, cashback) or more (paid search) and the leaving rate follows reading; an end-of-intro model without the full price is ruled out by the step-ups either side of April 2025; the customer number as the household is ruled out by the payment fingerprints and addresses that customer numbers share; and every reasonable variant of the reading measure, the tenure terms, the offer terms and the household links funds the same 8 lines.
