## Final Recommendation

**Commit EUR 10.0 million of December 2027 ARR in the 2027 plan, not the draft's EUR 10.3 million**: EUR 10.0 million is the largest EUR 0.1 million step met or beaten in at least 80 per cent of outcomes (83 in 100; EUR 10.1 million only 69 in 100), the draft is met about 36 times in 100, and expected ARR is EUR 10,220,000 with an 80 per cent range of EUR 9,940,000 to EUR 10,520,000.
