Step-by-step process.

Located and extracted input-assets/1.5.zip. I read the policy PDF growth_policy_paid_channels_v3_2026-09.pdf first and used it as the controlling rule: §2 install/return definitions, §2.3-2.4 revenue, §2.6 marginal return, §3.1 bands and §3.2 required placements fields, §4.1 November timing. Then I read media_plan_2027.xlsx (Growth channels, Stretch case, Brand) and paid_budgets_weekly_2024-2026.xlsx (Weekly budgets, Changes). The growth sheet covers 52 weeks for Meta iOS, TikTok iOS, Snapchat iOS, Google iOS, Pinterest iOS, Google Android and AppLovin Android; Stretch case has channel-specific stretch schedules; Brand is Apple Search Ads and, per §1.2, never placed. Plan week labels were mapped through actual ISO weeks to the 104-week record rather than treating plan ordinal 1 as record week 1.

Files used or rejected:

accounts_2024-11-04_2026-11-01.csv: 748,197 accounts and unique IDs; platform, storefront, account type, ATT status and the backend 48-hour conversion value. Used for install eligibility, tracking calibration and mature revenue joins.
attribution_installs_...csv: 771,674 install records. Used for Android, Apple Search Ads and ATT-authorized iOS; not treated as complete iOS truth.
skan_postbacks_...csv and skadnetwork_conversion_values.json: postbacks, conversion-value bits and 48-hour window/24-48-hour release timing. Used as iOS ground for first-install counts/value mix.
ad_spend_daily_...csv: 14,369 campaign-day records of billed spend, impressions/clicks and network-reported installs. Used actual spend and delivery ratio; network installs were not used as true installs.
billing_events_...csv: 500,694 events (500,225 production, 469 sandbox), the authoritative revenue source.
paid_budgets...xlsx: weekly set budgets and changes; used to distinguish budget from billed spend and to identify delivery-adjusted response, not as spend itself where actual bills differed.
Not used as channel truth: appstore_subscription_events...csv and googleplay_subscription_transactions...csv (aggregate store reports, not account charges); web_checkout_settlements...csv (monthly settlement aggregate, billing already has charge-level truth); creative_tests...csv (does not identify 12-month returns); store_ratings...csv (no policy consequence and not a return measure).
Install preparation: collapsed duplicate install identifiers by keeping the earliest event, then kept each account’s earliest install. This leaves 765,769 unique install IDs before account-first selection and 748,197 first installs, of which 748,034 are consumer accounts (115 staff and 48 test accounts excluded; later/reinstall events excluded). Media source was retained only where attribution identifies it: Android sources, Apple Search Ads, and ATT-authorized non-ASA iOS. For non-ASA iOS, duplicate postback IDs were collapsed and redownload=true postbacks removed; postback receipt lag (48-hour window plus 24-48 hours) was apportioned across install weeks using the 72-96-hour interval rather than treating network-reported installs as real. Apple Search Ads was removed from placement channels and used only as a validation holdout. Campaign IDs map exactly to spend countries/storefronts.

Revenue definition: for every first install, selected production billing events with transaction time t satisfying install time ≤ t < install time + 365 days, using transaction time rather than recording lag. Charge net revenue is amount/(1+VAT) less store commission 15% of VAT-net price for App Store/Google Play, or web processing fee 3%; VAT is ES 21%, IT 22%, PT 23%. Refunds of charges inside that window were deducted at the same net rate; sandbox events were excluded. This was computed per account, not inferred from subscription reports. “Year to 2 November 2025” was implemented as install weeks beginning 4 November 2024 through 27 October 2025, ending 2 November 2025 (52 weeks), because that is the latest fully matured install year at records through 8 November 2026; 2027 installs will not be twelve months old until 2028. I checked refund timing definitions and they did not change mature totals.

Why ATT-authorized iOS revenue is not channel revenue: tracking status is payment/retention linked even at equal 48-hour conversion value. On Android trial buckets, for example, authorized and denied users have materially different paid conversion and revenue-if-paid; authorized-only iOS attribution therefore selects higher-value users. Mature iOS channel RPI was built as (authorized installs × their directly observed mature revenue) plus (non-authorized installs × estimated revenue), divided by total true installs. Non-authorized counts are SKAdNetwork counts less directly attributed authorized first installs, by week × store × conversion value, with organic separated as the residual of all non-authorized first installs less paid non-authorized installs. Revenue means were deconvolved over weekly × store × conversion-value cells against all non-attributed installs, with organic as a separate component.

Response and quality: historical budgets are what was set; actual spend is budget × delivery ratio, and delivery concentrates in low-CPI/effective weeks, so raw billed-spend regression is not the causal plan response. For each channel I fit ISO-week season and delivery-adjusted log-log relationships using budget, log delivery ratio and a year term. Approximate planned-spend response exponents β were Meta 0.718, TikTok 0.838, Snapchat 0.758, Google iOS 0.664, Pinterest 0.656, Google Android 0.697 and AppLovin 0.788; log-log quality/RPI spend responses η were Meta −0.221, TikTok −0.224, Snapchat −0.209, Google iOS −0.179, Pinterest −0.275, Google Android −0.177 and AppLovin −0.172. Response-model residual scales were about 0.022-0.038 log points; quality residuals about 0.034-0.053. I also considered simpler alternatives: plain raw billed-spend OLS (rejected because delivery is efficiency-correlated and would overstate spend response), budget-as-instrument 2SLS (rejected as the plan model because it leaves delivery/efficiency variation in residuals at roughly 0.090-0.118 rather than recovering the near-deterministic delivery process and does not represent the plan’s stated spend level), and campaign-level reallocation models (campaigns explain only modest CPI/quality differences relative to channel-level spend/delivery and do not identify the policy’s channel placements).

2027 estimate: for each of the 52 ISO-mapped plan weeks, estimate installs from the channel response function at the plan’s stated weekly spend at delivery ratio 1.00, season carried from records and no drift beyond fitted history; estimate quality/RPI from the separate delivery-adjusted quality function and calibrate it to the channel’s mature-year truth (calibration factors roughly Meta 0.759, TikTok 0.743, Snapchat 0.835, Google iOS 1.239, Pinterest 1.139, Google Android 0.927, AppLovin 0.733). Weekly revenue is installs × RPI; return is summed revenue ÷ summed spend; plan RPI is summed revenue ÷ summed installs; average weekly installs is summed installs ÷ 52. Marginal return under §2.6 proportional scaling is (β + η) × average return, evaluated separately at plan and stretch. Stretch uses its own 52-week schedule, not plan plus stretch.

Checks: (a) ASA holdout - pretending Apple Search Ads non-authorized installs were unknown and re-solving the deconvolution recovered 11.96-12.30 € per non-authorized install against 11.871 € observed, about 1-4% high, while leaving the five growth channels stable. (b) Android semi-supervised deconvolution recovered denied-cohort returns within roughly single-digit percentages, with organic near 1.00, but noisier than iOS method used; EM two-part models were unstable/over-corrected and therefore not used. (c) Delivery-adjusted response residuals (0.022-0.038) were far below raw/IV specifications (0.090-0.118). (d) ISO-week versus plan-ordinal season mapping changed returns only in the third decimal and no band. (e) Mature Android returns are direct observations; iOS returns are estimates. Plan/scenario sensitivity is disclosed below because it is the one consequential ambiguity.

Assumptions and judgment calls.

Plan interpretation: I treat the media plan’s stated weekly values as the spend levels at which the insertion orders are to be placed, at delivery ratio 1.00, and apply the delivery-adjusted record model there. The plan gives no future delivery residual, so inventing one would be an unstated forecast; this also follows the plan’s own wording that the values are weekly spend as platforms bill it. This is a judgment call, not a fact in the data. A different reasonable treatment - reconciling drafted values to delivered spend through a delivery-ratio forecast, or using budget-instrument IV - leaves every band unchanged except Google Android, which falls below 0.90 and would be Cut. It does not change Google iOS/Pinterest Scale, Meta Hold, or TikTok/Snapchat/AppLovin Cut. Thus the recommendation is sensitive to plan-spend semantics only through Google Android; if Finance intends delivery-reconciled planned spend rather than the drafted spend levels as binding, that placement must be revisited before release. This sensitivity is disclosed in Basis & checks, “What is estimated vs observed.”

Seasonality is mapped by actual ISO week; plan and stretch are evaluated over their own 52 weeks; average installs/week is a schedule average. A constant-representative-week reading is not used because the plan varies spend by week and §2.1 defines a 52-week period.

No campaign-mix, price, tax, commission or plan-year drift beyond the 104-week record; 2027 prices/commissions per §2.5. SKAdNetwork is treated as true ad-driven first-install counts after deduplication/redownload removal, not as incremental proof beyond what records identify; “install an ad drove” is operationalized through attribution/SKAN ownership after removing organic residual, not a separate holdout experiment (none exists).

Mature-year iOS channel RPI is model-based deconvolution, not observation of each denied install’s channel. Changing deconvolution regularization within the stable range moves iOS non-authorized RPI only modestly and does not alter any Scale/Cut call; Google Android is directly observed but its plan placement is the delivery-semantics-sensitive case above.

Latest mature year is taken as 4 November 2024-2 November 2025 because the prompt names “year to 2 November 2025” and records end 8 November 2026. Using a literal trailing 365 days ending 8 November 2025 instead would alter mature benchmarks slightly, not policy bands.

Results are committed estimates under §4.1, not confidence intervals or guarantees; no external market/season data beyond records.

Alternatives rejected.
Raw ATT-authorized RPI as channel truth: rejected because authorized status selects payment propensity. Example: Meta’s authorized mature RPI is 9.161 € versus the channel’s estimated full-mix 5.105 €; TikTok authorized 6.190 € versus 3.689 €; Google iOS authorized 10.363 € versus 8.669 €. Using authorized figures would overstate Meta/TikTok/Snap and distort placements.

Directly observed organic/partner revenue as paid-channel return, or treating SKAN as merely reported installs: rejected because SKAN is the only near-complete iOS ad-driven install source after ATT; network-reported installs overstate true iOS first installs by roughly 8-22% by channel and are not used.

Delivery-ignored budget OLS and budget-instrument IV as the placement model: rejected on residual/fit grounds (about 0.090-0.118 log points versus 0.022-0.038 delivery-adjusted) and because naive billed-spend regression confounds spend with efficiency/delivery. They remain relevant only as the disclosed Google Android sensitivity, not as the committed model.

Creative tests, ratings and store/settlement aggregates as return evidence: rejected for lack of install-level 365-day net revenue/channel identification; billing events are policy truth.

Placing on mature-year RPI alone: rejected; §3.1 places on 2027 return at plan spend. E.g. Google Android mature RPI 6.611 € versus plan 7.455 €, AppLovin 3.136 € versus 3.337 €, but bands still require installs/spend response and quality at plan scale.

Evidence against the call: Google Android’s 0.977 € / € is only 0.077 above the Hold floor, and delivery-reconciled/IV treatments push it under 0.90. I do not change the call because the committed model is the delivery-adjusted plan-spend specification with materially smaller residuals and because the plan/policy draft is treated as binding spend levels; the sensitivity is explicitly escalated rather than hidden. Google iOS and Pinterest remain Scale under all three treatments; Meta remains Hold; the three Cut channels remain Cut.
