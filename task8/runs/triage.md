## Evidence: why the deep step was reachable (for re-rooting)
1. **The prompt carries the gap.** It asks for the November 2027 sitting and the regional asks name the August 2026 sitting. A calibration sitting and a use sitting months apart, in a school subject, prompts the first question a model asks: what changes between August and November? In education the answer is teaching. Both runs open the coverage step from that gap ("Aligned August evidence with November coverage"; "The approved November window is 1-12 November 2027").
2. **The course outline states the clock.** Its first paragraph files "six core units over Terms 1 to 3", with the Term 3 units at "4 to 6" weeks suggested and a table column `Term`. An August sitting is mid-Term 3, so it falls before U5 and U6 are finished. The design treated the outline's model weeks as rung 2 (A), but neither run stopped there. The outline put the mechanism in front of them and the appendix gave the per-school join.
3. **Appendix B works as a work order.** The note's "Second use" says the course review is filed for its unit-to-strand map. That map is the section 1 table. Appendix B (each school's Term 3 start date, its own week numbers, its Term 3 sequence, with the header "Week numbers are the school's own weeks of the term, counted from the school's Term 3 start date") has no use in the pack except the decisive join. Its header also closes SF12, so the rung 3 trap (national calendar) caught neither run: run 1 "including Tuesday starts", run 2 tested a Monday-aligned reading. This is the "anything written down is executed" law. A per-school sequence table in a pre-equating task is read as an instruction to compute opportunity to learn.
4. **The specification and minute aim at the taught population.** Spec section 4 files "for a candidate sitting the 2027 paper", and the minute moves the sitting to 1 to 12 November. Run 2 joined them in one sentence: "The course review places all six core units before November". The estimand pin that determinism needs is the same sentence that points to the remedy.
5. **The signal is large and clean once the join is made.** Run 1 measured the untaught shift at about 1.0 logit per unit with tight intervals (0.84 to 1.10, 0.96 to 1.19), and out-of-sample gains in every fold. Run 2 had C in 990 of 1,000 bootstraps. No second explanation competes (no confound between taught state and ability that the runs had to separate), so the "modeling judgment" took one inference: drop or flag untaught responses. Opportunity to learn and item parameter invariance is textbook IRT (DIF by instruction). By the house law, "the field's named method is the opponent's first move", it sits at rung 1, not rung 4.
6. **Both runs ran the rung-0 bank and the pooled refit and named them as rejected alternatives.** The runs did not miss the bank route. They used it as a control. The "silent" readability verdict in the note was wrong on columns 1 and 3: the outline plus the appendix is a filed clause in practice, and opportunity to learn is a reflex in this field.
7. **The asks gave no loss.** Each ask device (host codes against home school D7, unrostered walk-ins D4, office aliases D1, the percentage-keyed batch D3 with the roster header filing "the mark out of 50 and as a percentage ... each keyer's session as one batch on one template") is a device whose discovery is its remedy. Runs that spend 27 to 40 minutes and read 90 to 126 resources run the hygiene battery completely.
