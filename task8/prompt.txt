What pass mark should the Board set on its 2027 Level 1 Numeracy paper, given that the qualification specification makes the pass mark the mark a candidate who just meets the Level 1 standard can be expected to score on that paper? The Awards Committee wants the recommendation in pass_mark_2027.docx, and l1_2027_items.xlsx should hold one row for each item on the 2027 paper with the chance that a candidate who just meets the standard answers it correctly and whether that chance sits inside the specification's targeting band, together with, for each learning centre region, how many of the candidates who sat the 2026 Level 1 paper there would be expected to pass the 2027 paper. Pass marks and expected passes are whole numbers and chances are to two decimal places.
