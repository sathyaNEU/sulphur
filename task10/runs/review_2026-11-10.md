at the primary across an open point and read at minus 3.0 C).

## The platform's holistic audit (verbatim, received 2026-11-10)

> Your demand side is solid work. Getting from duplicated, renamed SCADA points, revised weather and revised
> instructions to a corrected, grown design-day demand reproduces cleanly (30.1, 25.1, 36.1 and 23.0 MW), and
> modelling provider delivery as a function of temperature is a real method step.
>
> The problem is that the call turns on one built-in factor. Tollbrook Lane, Wexley Hill and Kestwick Fields step up
> by a flat 1.4, 1.4 and 0.8 MW on their zone's event days, whatever the MW scheduled. The step is absent on
> cancelled days and at every other neighbour. Netting it alone moves Hartwell from 17.5 MW (within firm) to 20.3 MW
> (over firm). Once someone is told "the open-point neighbours pick up load during events", the fix is one
> subtraction. A solver with a careful temperature-dependent model but no netting commits Ashcombe Road only. So the
> answer depends on spotting the plant, not on analysis.
>
> To fix it, document the transfer, for example as switching log entries, and make the call turn on judgments that
> each move a figure:
> - the shape of the cold-weather delivery decline, validated on held-out events;
> - whether delivery scales to awards 1.5 to 2.5 times the largest historical dose;
> - how to carry baseline uncertainty to Hartwell's margin.
>
> For what we are looking for, see https://project-sulfur.learn.joinhandshake.com/nov-09-updates.

## Verdict

REDESIGN, on the review. Both runs lost at the deciding step for a reasoning reason, and the audit still reads the
step as a plant: the neighbour's rise is one inserted factor, one sentence names it and one subtraction then lands
the call. The world, the demand side and the provider's temperature response are kept (the audit calls them solid and
a real method step); the transfer becomes documented texture in a switching log, and the build is re-rooted as a
results chain whose difficulty sits in the judgments the audit lists, each of which the record has to settle and each
of which moves a figure.
