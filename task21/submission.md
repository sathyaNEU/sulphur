**Raise comparison-site new-business rates from 1 January 2027 by 27 per cent for drivers under 25 and 7 per cent for drivers aged 25 to 64, and drop the 3 per cent over-65 cut.** The team's 21 per cent, which the loss-ratio method also gives, leaves the under-25 business it would win at a 79.0 per cent loss ratio because a higher price keeps only the risks the rating model under-priced most; the latest twelve months' 26 per cent for 25-to-64s carries business the old model priced; and the over-65 cut's £0.62 million on new business is outweighed by £1.99 million lost on renewals capped at the lower new-business price, £1.37 million less underwriting profit in all.
