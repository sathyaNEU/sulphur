Which of our internal services ran over their 2026 latency error budget? Each gets a reliability engineer from January to June 2027, starting with the one whose SLI counts the most requests over its threshold. In a short report, name them in that order with each one's share of the over-threshold requests all our services' SLIs count and a 90 per cent confidence interval on its SLI, give every service's SLI beside its budget with a chart of the two, name the service that owned the root cause of each customer-facing incident in December, and say how many more over-threshold requests the services that ran over served than their budgets allow. In a CSV, give each service its SLI, budget, whether it ran over and its customer-facing incident minutes in each half of 2026, counting it from when the incident tool added it. SLIs and budgets are per cents to one decimal, intervals per cents to two, shares whole per cents, minutes whole and requests to the nearest ten thousand.
