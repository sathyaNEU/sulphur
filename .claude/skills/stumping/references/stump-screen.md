# The stump screen: on paper, before any code

Most of a returned build's cost was spent building an idea that a careful run's reflexes, a filed sentence or the
client's closed page had already given away. This screen kills those ideas in fifteen minutes, before the card is
registered and before a line of the generator exists. The platform's two runs are the only measure of difficulty;
nothing in-house solves the task, so the idea has to be right before it is built.

## How to run it

0. Put the world through test 0 below before any trap is written. A world that fails it dies in one line,
   whatever its traps would have been.
1. Write three to five candidate traps for the draw, one line each: the part of the decision's quantity that no
   record holds and the observation process that hides it, the parameter a run must set to reach the figure and the
   default it reaches for (a pooled value, today's level, a literature constant, the same as another group,
   independence, a pro-rata split, the face value), the second record that refutes the default without scoring the
   parameter, and what the hurried route, the direct route, the most rigorous standard route and the four run routes
   (generous, calibrated, hedge, cut-off: test 19) each file. A candidate that is a mechanism of the world the rows carry (a penalty, a response
   shape, a signature, a loss term) is modelled on sight and is written as a supporting result, never as a trap
   (`../SKILL.md` Part 1d).
2. Put each candidate through the twenty tests below. One NO drops it; do not repair a candidate by writing a sentence
   into a document.
3. Keep the three strongest that are independent of one another (no one sentence of help rescues two of them) and
   write the table into the design note's `## Stump screen` section: each candidate, its twenty answers, kept or
   dropped and why.
4. Only then register the card, prototype the numbers in a scratch script and write the generator.

## Test 0: the world, before any trap is written

Name the estimand (population, members not yet observed included, outcome, window, unit) and list every record a
real file of that kind ships, with the public sources that show it (no private or project detail in any query).
Then read the outcome as it stands before any option or forward change is applied. The world dies if any of these
holds:

- (a) a shipped monitor, gauge, meter, register or routine sampling programme observes that outcome, or a monotone
  function of it, or a quantity the field's reference method turns into it with no judgment left (a reservoir's
  temperature chains through the energy budget, land surface temperature through the station calibration), at a
  deciding member over the history window, continuously or on a schedule (an instrument that sees only a part of the
  member, only the units that chose or were chosen to be recorded, or whose recording depends on conditions of how
  the record is made that no shipped check calibrates, is a partial instrument and does not count);
- (b) an account, a mass balance, or a simple sum or difference of shipped records closes on a deciding member's
  total or on the part of it the decision moves;
- (c) the option's lever is a shipped series and the outcome is observed at the deciding members. A forward or
  counterfactual estimand does not escape (a): the strongest route regresses the observed outcome on the lever, with
  any lags, interactions with a logged state and running totals since a logged reset (all of them test 18's
  regression specifications on named fields), and prices the option.

A region-wide or network-wide total may ship, but only as the falsification check (test 16). The world passes only
where each instrument sees a different part of the estimand and none closes the whole: the approved builds' anatomy
(`CLAUDE.md`, the four approved builds), stated as a gate. Where it fails, the strongest route calibrates end to end
and every trap dies at test 15 or 18 however good it is. Every world task3 architecture 11 priced in arithmetic died
that way: the coater's solvent account (b), the effluent works' scheduled grabs, the radon zones' monitors, the
valley SO2 monitors, the construction-erosion storm gauges, the river reaches' ammonia sondes, and the rail yard's
six monitors, asked for an annual mean and again for hours over an action level (all (a), the last two with (c)). A
first wording, whether any record observes the estimand itself, let the river and rail worlds through, because no
record observes a 2027 counterfactual; the wording above kills all seven on its literal text.

## The twenty tests (every kept trap answers YES to all twenty)

1. **Off the closed page.** Not one of the client's 45 closed strategies (`guidelines/client/learning-modules/
   overused-strategies-2026-11-21.md`; same mechanism and the same kind of fix is the same lesson, whatever the
   domain). Write the candidate's fix in one clause and read it against each card's "the fix already used": a
   gross-up by a share from an independent source, a factor from checked cases, a basis restated, a curve fitted and
   valued at the margin, a knock-on carried period by period, a without-it baseline, a shape learned from units
   that reached the range and a held-out score are all cards (`open-ground.md` Part 1). A closed lesson can only
   support: its omission alone leaves the golden call standing. Every candidate is read first against the
   calibrated-source card by its fix ("measure the bias on cases where both the source and a checked value exist,
   and correct every reading with it (by group where the bias varies)"; same mechanism and the same kind of fix):
   a bias or recording effect sized on cases where the source and a checked value both exist is that card, by group
   or not, however the factor is then applied; a single recording effect fitted on one named field or one-join
   covariate (a time since sampling, a logged condition) is test 18's strongest route where a shipped file checks it,
   and is priced on test 19's run routes where nothing does; two or more recording effects
   that bias each other's estimate when each is fitted alone, with no checked sample, separated by one joint fit and
   proved on a signal the recording conditions cannot move, are open (`open-ground.md`, recording effects tangled
   together; the client's example 17, L56 to L58), and so is a recording stump whose estimator is a conservation
   residual, a recording algorithm inverted or a state the record never held. task3 arch 11's effluent candidate
   (ammonia lost from a sample waiting warm in its bottle, gained from a sample line that stood) is the single-effect
   case: each effect fitted alone on its own logged condition landed every member (weff4.py to weff9.py), so such a
   world lives only where nothing shipped checks either correction.
2. **Not a reflex.** No move a careful run makes unprompted settles it (`precedents.json` reflexes: completing
   reporting lags, de-duplicating, a constant-unit panel, re-weighting to the target's mix, the field's textbook
   estimator, hold-outs, back-tests, bootstraps). If that battery executed perfectly lands inside the band, it is not
   a trap. **The field's whole textbook chain is one reflex when the pack ships its ingredients**, however many steps
   it has: task22 arch 1's runs replayed the switching log, rebuilt the breaker gaps and computed current squared hour
   by hour on the GIS (R38 to R42); task7 arch 12's run read a partially complied voltage trial on the achieved dose
   with IV, carried it to every feeder and hour by thermostatic share from the survey and the load research, set the
   volts from the far-end meters hour by hour and priced losses at the lowered voltage (R67 to R70), all four priced
   as stumps from other builds' losses (N12, N21, N25, L15). A precedent counts only where the run lost with the
   ingredients in front of it; a pack that ships a survey, a published draw per unit and a trial has shipped the
   textbook transport.
3. **Nothing filed gives it away.** No sentence in any shipped document names the mechanism, the step, the window,
   the population or the cause. Documents describe the business; the analysis is found in the data.
4. **Clean-data test.** With every file complete and correctly labelled, the call still needs an estimation
   judgment: a stock rebuilt from its flows, a response measured on the units that show it, a functional of a path,
   a residual attributed. A trap whose remedy is a filter, a join, a union or a re-key is Layer 1, never a stump.
5. **Two records, two grains.** The evidence sits across two records at different grains or times (a balance-sheet
   stock against a weekly flow, a monthly descriptor share against a quarterly ledger, a dated operations log against
   a register). No single plot or group-by of one file shows the answer.
6. **An assumption the run names and waves through.** Name it and cite its precedent (N02 history taken as
   informative of the forward window; N13 the latest rate taken as the end state; N01 an enlarged load assumed to
   scale in proportion; L07 a measurement change flagged and never tested; L37 a check that cannot see the
   condition; L40 to L42 a fitted form or effect held constant past the data's support; L43 and L44 a deferral
   where the bridge has to be built; N42 one instrument model's correction put on another for want of a check). The
   world's process makes it false for the target and weaker in the history, so the run's own back-test still passes.
   **A precedent transfers only with its condition**: every named assumption on file was waved through because no
   shipped file let the run score it. task3 arch 8 cited N21, N26, N01 and N30 for three stumps whose checks it
   shipped (the storage gauge's account, the radar's pixel pairs, the research valley's gauges), and both runs made
   all three (R98 to R100). A precedent cited for a stump with a validation handle (test 18) prices nothing.
7. **A plausible shallower story.** The data offers a wrong explanation that passes its own checks (L26), so a run
   that notices the pattern still files the wrong number.
8. **Across the data.** The process runs through the history at different sizes, never only in the deciding units,
   and one sentence about it does not leave one routine step.
9. **Worth the call, as a multiple.** Alone, it moves its member across the decision line by three times the golden
   family's spread or more, and its shortcut lands on a different wrong answer from the other traps' shortcuts. On
   the approved sheets of 2026-11-14 the shortcut's figure sat two to four times the golden's (1.33 against 0.46 per
   cent; +0.30 and +0.625 against -0.04) while the accepted bands ran to a third or a half of the estimate either
   side; our forcing returns priced traps worth a few per cent against lines a few per cent away. The spread is the variant
   battery's (`determinism-check` §1: every convention the golden uses, rebuilt the ways the field does it), measured
   before the trap is priced; task7 arch 12's battery moved residential feeders 11 to 23 percent and gave 11 funded
   sets in 96 variants, wider than two of its stumps.
10. **The run's alternative is ruled out by named data.** For each trap, write the route a careful run would take
   instead and the file and figure that refute it. "Defensible but wrong" is the platform auditor's
   `defensible_alternative_rejected`: the run's miss is then not evidence of difficulty and the result is a fork.
11. **The golden can measure it.** The correction the trap needs is estimated from the cut pack at the precision the
   call needs, measured in a prototype before the card is registered. task7's compressor energy response read off
   load tap changer steps came out at -0.21 (0.22) over 16,179 events against a true -0.30, too loose to pin any
   feeder, and died there.
12. **No easier second route.** Read the pack's most direct evidence for the trap at face value: count the documented
   cases, read the confirming file raw, copy the confirmed reference cases' signature. It must land wrong. task16 v3's
   twins sat in the delivery log and its false links in the blood typing, and task13 v5's grow-ins matched the patrol
   reports to the hour, so the runs never needed the model (`../SKILL.md` Part 3.4, rule 2).
13. **It opens its own member.** The trap moves a member of the call that no other kept trap moves and that the
   reflex route files wrong, and the member's margin sits between the honest family's spread and the trap's worth.
   Three traps acting on one member open one member: task1 v5 had three, and only Onbeperkt was in play
   (`../SKILL.md` Part 3.4, rules 1 and 3).

14. **Said out loud.** Write the trap's problem in one sentence, as if the prompt carried it. What is left must
   still be an estimator the run has to construct: two or more records, three or more dependent steps, no named
   method whose inputs ship ready. On the approved A/B sheet the run had the sentence unprompted (the labelled rows
   are mostly the bad outcomes, R91) and filed every figure as not available (L53); on the survey sheet the sentence
   "net the reversals" still left linkage, weighting, annualisation, the timing check, the condition with no timing
   item and the older group's break. A trap that dies when said out loud was a trap of noticing, which five Sulfur
   rounds landed.
15. **The strongest route lands wrong.** Besides the hurried choice and the direct route (test 12), write the most
   rigorous standard design the pack affords for this member (a difference against the units not yet treated with a
   placebo, a model fitted on the labelled sample and carried across, the textbook correction with its interval) and
   the file and figure that refute it. The review's Stumped gate rests on its single strongest run, and that run
   builds this design unprompted (R92): it lost only where the design's comparison units, calibration or record
   failed for a reason another record shows and its own placebo could not see (L51), where it saw the bias and
   declined to estimate (L53, L43), or where it took a recorded change for the event (L54). If the rigorous route
   lands inside the band, the member is forced.
16. **A referee or a falsification check, never a key and never a calibration set.** Something shipped rules between
   estimators, or the call is unforced. Either a slice where the hidden quantity is known or bounded and which does
   not cover the target (a random labelled sample in one arm or one month, a dated item present for some members,
   earlier periods the treatment never reached, a complete count for one stratum), or a falsification check: a
   statistic of the members' own records that the golden's model reproduces and each rival's misses, with no file
   observing the target (example 17's qualifying share within the households that say they are falling behind,
   rising across the deadline bands while their share of the sample stays flat; the leave-one-out and leave-two-out
   table beside it). **A referee is a calibration set first**: task3 arch 8's runs never read the research valley's
   gauges raw; they calibrated the whole chain on them end to end, conditioned on the radar's ratios, integrated the
   residual and filed every class (R101). So a slice that observes the target, or a stump's own quantity, under the
   members' instruments is shipped only where the calibrated run route on it files the wrong call (test 19), and
   otherwise the falsification check forces the call. In the prototype the golden reproduces the slice or the
   statistic and each rival misses it by a stated figure. A fork a referee or a falsification check closes is still
   a stump; a fork a filed convention closes is a rule (task24's forecasting convention, task20's filed bases).
17. **One sentence does not cover the members.** Either a second, independent trap opens another member (test 13), or
   this trap's own correction has a domain of validity the members cross: its identifying assumption is checked on
   some members, cannot be checked on one (decided by a bound and its break-even) and fails its check on another
   (decided on a second basis the same records support). Name which, with the figures. The arrangement is drawn from
   this world's records and is never the one a build on file used. The family sentence is priced as well: the one
   sentence that names what the kept stumps share rescues a third of the rubric or less on the reflex route, or the
   stumps are one lesson however different their records (task3 arch 11's coater pair: "the certificates are
   test-day figures; the gas meters and the waste manifests show what each line did" rescued about 75 points
   against a ceiling of 33). Two recording effects on one instrument are two traps when each has its own recording
   condition (its own covariate), its own fitted figure and its own member, no checked sample ships, and the sentence
   that names their joint fit, handed to the reflex route at full strength, rescues a third of the rubric or less: a
   sentence that names a method the data force while each effect's construction is still the run's to build does not
   make the task routine (the client's one-fact exception, Holistic Audit v4 Part D; example 17 counts deadline
   recording and interview length on one survey as two decisive steps).
18. **No validation handle.** No shipped file lets a run score the stump's own quantity, or the target, under the
   members' instruments: a total that closes the loss, a pair that reads the ratio off, a slice that scores the
   chain, a confirming log that counts the cases. Where one exists the run fits the correction and checks it there
   (task3 arch 8: the tipping time fitted through the storage gauge's account, R98; each source's ratio read off the
   radar's pixel pairs, R99; hail calibrated on the research valley's weighing gauges, R100). The approved runs and
   the client's hardest examples lost where nothing scores the correction: they saw the signal and kept it as a
   sensitivity ("not a uniquely identified correction"), cut at a threshold, picked one arm, or declined to estimate
   (L53, examples 16 and 17). So the parameter is identified only through a covariate the run must build from
   another file (days to each interview's own deadline, the queue a case opened under, each customer's use count
   across two systems) or through an internal consistency check, and test 16's falsification check is how the
   golden shows it; a file that measures the parameter for some units is the closed calibration card (test 1). A
   stump is live only when (a) a run that fits every free parameter on the pack as shipped cannot recover it without
   a covariate built from a second file or a joint fit with a tangled neighbour, (b) its default passes the back-test
   a run does on the history, because the world's process is weaker there, and (c) the process that moves the forward
   window is not a closed card's fix. No collocated pair ships for any stump's parameter (two heights or two sensors
   at one site, a total beside a tally): that pairing was R98's and R99's handle. **The strongest run's route is priced
   at full strength on the shipped fields wherever a shipped file lets a run check the correction** (a validation
   handle, a calibration slice, a target or an account observed at the members: test 0) **and on every mechanism of
   the world the rows carry**: every field the pack names and every covariate that one join, group-by, filter or flag
   of a shipped log gives, fitted jointly, and that route files the wrong call or the stump is a reflex. Every
   correction on file that a run made unprompted had such a check (R09, R15, R41, R57, R66, R68, R74, R98, R100,
   R101). An observation-process correction that nothing shipped checks is priced on test 19's run routes instead
   (kept as a sensitivity, cut at a threshold, declined, applied generously), because that is where the runs on
   record lost it (L56, L57, L53; `CLAUDE.md`: they "lose where nothing checks the correction"), so a time to or
   since a logged event (days to each interview's own deadline) prices at full strength only where a shipped file
   checks the correction it carries. task3 arch 11's coater pair died on it: the oxidiser's daily gas regressed on each line's sprayed solvent
   with fan hours and ambient called 8.5 of 9 lines right, and the booth sludge split the same way, so a conservation
   account whose every term ships is the runs' (R98, task22 arch 1). The golden needs (a) a covariate built from a
   second file by a construction that is not a join, union, filter, stratification or re-key (days to each
   interview's own deadline, the queue a case opened under, each customer's use count across two systems), or (b) a
   joint fit of two effects that each bias the other when fitted alone, one of them not a named field (a default
   multiple regression already fits two named ones), and each such loss cites a precedent where a run was seen one
   move short (L56 to L67). No field a real record of that kind carries is withheld to keep a covariate unbuilt: a
   laboratory extract carries its received and analysed times, and task3 arch 11's effluent candidate held only with
   them withheld (any later timestamp recovered the bottle's loss on 19 of 19 members). The note's `Validation handles:`
   line is the matrix of every shipped file against every stump's parameter. **A near-complete slice beside a filed
   recording law is a calibration set** even when no file holds the target: thinned through the law, the slice
   simulates the record, and any calibration matched to each member's own distribution recovers the stump (task3
   arch 9's first cores: the well-covered days thinned through the vendor's curve, with one donor cut per station
   matched to its own recorded storm sizes, filed the golden's call in 24 of 27 settings, so the golden's unfold
   differed from the run's ratio in precision only). Such a stump is identified from the thin records' own internal
   variation (the same units seen at two recording levels, the record's own timing), never from a slice that sees
   the process whole. **And a filed recording law is itself a handle**: with the law in the pack, a run degrades its
   own record further through it (thins it to fewer sensors, adds the filed error), fits how the figure moves with
   the depth of recording, and extrapolates to full sight. task3 arch 9's donor-free rebuilds were determinate across
   seven and eight storm families and still landed: a log ladder of hours on the share located filed the golden's
   call at 11 of 12 settings, and with flash structure the ladder scaled by the textbook flash formula at 7 of 16,
   because the full record is the smooth limit of the record at successive depths. So a live recording stump turns
   on an unfiled parameter of the recording (what an older receiver adds, which no document states), identified by a
   joint fit or by a covariate built from another file, and no near-complete subsample hands that parameter over
   (the strong strokes every network locates put a station's true count within ten per cent and read the receiver's
   worth off, the warning case beside it). **An unfiled parameter is still a closed card when its fix is the card's**: a
   bias sized on cases where the source and a checked value both exist, by group or not, is the calibrated-source
   card (test 1), and a shield's solar gain, a gauge's undercatch or a sensor's offset sized that way is that card
   whatever else the record carries (task3 arch 10's first screen also handed the recognition over in its frame); a
   quantity read off as the residual of a conservation account (what a register cannot otherwise explain, task4's
   lost containers) is another kind of fix, and conservation stays open ground; and a frame with the history on one sensor basis and the decision
   on another hands every run the recognition in the prompt, so two such parameters are never independent.
19. **The runs' routes land wrong, on both sides of a line.** Beside the hurried, direct and rigorous routes, write
   the four routes frontier runs take and what each files: the generous route (every correction the pack suggests at
   full strength, the estimate's uncertainty integrated: task3 arch 8's runs summed exceedance probabilities over a
   fitted spread, which lifts a tail count above a point plug), the calibrated route (end to end on any slice that
   observes the target or a stump's quantity), the hedge route (every signal noticed, reported as a sensitivity, the
   face value central: L53, examples 16 and 17) and the cut-off route (a filter where a fitted model is needed:
   example 17's best run kept the interviews more than 14 days out). Each files a wrong call, and the misses across
   them and the shortcuts fall on both sides of a line: at least one member where over-correcting crosses it and one
   where under-correcting does, so no blanket adjustment lands the call, and each stump on its own: pushed past its
   fitted value to the far end a run might set, it files a wrong call too. A route with a free setting is priced at
   every setting (every cut of the filter that replaces the stump's fit, every slice a calibration could be matched
   on), because the run picks the setting that matches its target (task3 arch 9's first core: the well-covered days
   kept to the thin stations' storm size filed the golden's call at cuts from 5 to 12 strokes). task3 arch 8 sat every open member 1.1 to
   1.5 storms above its line with every priced shortcut under it, and the runs' generous corrections filed all seven
   classes. `core.py` implements the four (`run_routes`) and `corelib.py` refuses a core where one files the right
   call, earns over 50, or the misses fall on one side only. The strongest arrangement of two stumps corrects in
   opposite directions on shared members, so each fixed alone moves the call to the other wrong side: the run that
   solves one lands across the line one way and the run that solves neither the other way (L06, L63, one step fitted
   and the other declined).
20. **A forward window.** The estimand is never the record's own mean or rate restated: the window's figure differs
   from what the history holds by a process of the world the data measures, and the history-mean route files a
   stated figure against the golden's. task3 arch 8's regulation defined the storms a season as the mean over the
   record's warm seasons, so the 2027 to 2031 programme did no work and the task reduced to correcting the record.

## Where to look first

`open-ground.md` §4 lists the dimensions the client's closed page never reaches. One prompt each:

- **Hidden state**: what stock sits between what is measured and what is booked (deposits, backlogs, queues,
  inventories, charge levels)?
- **Conservation**: which account must close, and what is its residual?
- **Structure that changes**: which membership moved over the history, and which dated record moved it?
