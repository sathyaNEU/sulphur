# The stump screen: on paper, before any code

Most of a returned build's cost was spent building an idea that a careful run's reflexes, a filed sentence or the
client's closed page had already given away. This screen kills those ideas in fifteen minutes, before the card is
registered and before a line of the generator exists. The platform's two runs are the only measure of difficulty;
nothing in-house solves the task, so the idea has to be right before it is built.

## How to run it

1. Write three to five candidate traps for the draw, one line each: the world's process that makes it, the
   assumption a careful run states and moves on from, the second record that refutes the assumption, and what the
   hurried route files.
2. Put each candidate through the eleven tests below. One NO drops it; do not repair a candidate by writing a sentence
   into a document.
3. Keep the three strongest that are independent of one another (no one sentence of help rescues two of them) and
   write the table into the design note's `## Stump screen` section: each candidate, its eleven answers, kept or
   dropped and why.
4. Only then register the card, prototype the numbers in a scratch script and write the generator.

## The thirteen tests (every kept trap answers YES to all thirteen)

1. **Off the closed page.** Not one of the client's 34 closed strategies (`guidelines/client/learning-modules/
   overused-strategies-2026-11-12.md`; same mechanism and the same kind of fix is the same lesson, whatever the
   domain). A closed lesson can only support: its omission alone leaves the golden call standing.
2. **Not a reflex.** No move a careful run makes unprompted settles it (`precedents.json` reflexes: completing
   reporting lags, de-duplicating, a constant-unit panel, re-weighting to the target's mix, the field's textbook
   estimator, hold-outs, back-tests, bootstraps). If that battery executed perfectly lands inside the band, it is not
   a trap. **The field's whole textbook chain is one reflex when the pack ships its ingredients**, however many steps
   it has: task22 arch 1's runs replayed the switching log, rebuilt the breaker gaps and computed current squared hour
   by hour on the GIS (R38 to R42); task7 arch 12's run read a partially complied voltage trial on the achieved dose
   with IV, carried it to every feeder and hour by thermostatic share from the survey and the load research, set the
   volts from the far-end meters hour by hour and priced losses at the lowered voltage (R67 to R70), all four priced
   as stumps from other builds' losses (N12, N21, N25, L15). A precedent counts only where the run lost with the
   ingredients in front of it; a pack that ships a survey, a published draw per unit and a trial has shipped the
   textbook transport.
3. **Nothing filed gives it away.** No sentence in any shipped document names the mechanism, the step, the window,
   the population or the cause. Documents describe the business; the analysis is found in the data.
4. **Clean-data test.** With every file complete and correctly labelled, the call still needs an estimation
   judgment: a stock rebuilt from its flows, a response measured on the units that show it, a functional of a path,
   a residual attributed. A trap whose remedy is a filter, a join, a union or a re-key is Layer 1, never a stump.
5. **Two records, two grains.** The evidence sits across two records at different grains or times (a balance-sheet
   stock against a weekly flow, a monthly descriptor share against a quarterly ledger, a dated operations log against
   a register). No single plot or group-by of one file shows the answer.
6. **An assumption the run names and waves through.** Name it and cite its precedent (N02 history taken as
   informative of the forward window; N13 the latest rate taken as the end state; N01 an enlarged load assumed to
   scale in proportion; L07 a measurement change flagged and never tested; L37 a check that cannot see the
   condition; L40 to L42 a fitted form or effect held constant past the data's support; L43 and L44 a deferral
   where the bridge has to be built). The world's process makes it false for the target and weaker in the history, so the run's own
   back-test still passes.
7. **A plausible shallower story.** The data offers a wrong explanation that passes its own checks (L26), so a run
   that notices the pattern still files the wrong number.
8. **Across the data.** The process runs through the history at different sizes, never only in the deciding units,
   and one sentence about it does not leave one routine step.
9. **Worth the call.** Alone, it moves the answer across the decision line by more than the golden family's spread,
   and its shortcut lands on a different wrong answer from the other traps' shortcuts. The spread is the variant
   battery's (`determinism-check` §1: every convention the golden uses, rebuilt the ways the field does it), measured
   before the trap is priced; task7 arch 12's battery moved residential feeders 11 to 23 percent and gave 11 funded
   sets in 96 variants, wider than two of its stumps.
10. **The run's alternative is ruled out by named data.** For each trap, write the route a careful run would take
   instead and the file and figure that refute it. "Defensible but wrong" is the platform auditor's
   `defensible_alternative_rejected`: the run's miss is then not evidence of difficulty and the result is a fork.
11. **The golden can measure it.** The correction the trap needs is estimated from the cut pack at the precision the
   call needs, measured in a prototype before the card is registered. task7's compressor energy response read off
   load tap changer steps came out at -0.21 (0.22) over 16,179 events against a true -0.30, too loose to pin any
   feeder, and died there.
12. **No easier second route.** Read the pack's most direct evidence for the trap at face value: count the documented
   cases, read the confirming file raw, copy the confirmed reference cases' signature. It must land wrong. task16 v3's
   twins sat in the delivery log and its false links in the blood typing, and task13 v5's grow-ins matched the patrol
   reports to the hour, so the runs never needed the model (`../SKILL.md` Part 3.4, rule 2).
13. **It opens its own member.** The trap moves a member of the call that no other kept trap moves and that the
   reflex route files wrong, and the member's margin sits between the honest family's spread and the trap's worth.
   Three traps acting on one member open one member: task1 v5 had three, and only Onbeperkt was in play
   (`../SKILL.md` Part 3.4, rules 1 and 3).

## The evidence behind the tests (task20, the card-panel nowcast, two blind judge passes)

- **Lost by both passes**: revenue recognised on delivery against card payments, the undelivered orders a stock no
  record holds (customer deposits on the balance sheet against custom-order descriptors by month): one pass noted the
  deposits and left them as "sensitivity only" (L07). A block of accounts re-issued under an issuer present in both
  quarters: the second pass read the step-up as a new studio (L26). The first passes 6 and 7 here, but it sits next to
  the closed "survey changed" card, so it could only support.
- **Earned by both passes by reflex or by a filed sentence**: completing the latest weeks, taking the licensees out
  (a 10-K sentence), re-weighting to the company's regions, a contract spike visible in one table, the back-test's
  summary error. Every one of them was priced as difficulty at some point and every one was given away.

## Where to look first

`open-ground.md` §4 lists the dimensions the client's closed page never reaches. One prompt each:

- **Hidden state**: what stock sits between what is measured and what is booked (deposits, backlogs, queues,
  inventories, charge levels)?
- **Conservation**: which account must close, and what is its residual?
- **Structure that changes**: which membership moved over the history, and which dated record moved it?
- **Aggregation**: is the quantity a peak, a quantile or a ratio of sums that the parts' own figures misstate?
- **Nonlinear path**: does the outcome turn on a square, a threshold or a maximum of a path that averages hide?
- **Network position**: does where a unit sits change what the same load does?
- **The recording algorithm**: which stored values are an algorithm's output (estimation and editing, a rolling
  register, a billing cycle) that has to be inverted before anything is measured?
- **Instruments with different blind spots**: which part of the estimand does each source see, and which part does
  none of them see?
- **The constant the run will hold**: which quantity will a careful run hold fixed where the target sits (an effect
  over time, an uplift across levels, a response past the obvious channel's plateau, an event's trace in final
  values), does the world's process move it there, and which slice of the data tests it with the first channel held
  fixed?
