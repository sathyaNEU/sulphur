Step-by-step process.

Located the sole input asset, 9.1.zip, extracted it, and read the charter first. Paper SLG-26-19 defines Q3 as 1 July-30 September 2026 by the mart's business dates; seven registry groups; conversion in per cent with differences in percentage points; normal service as scheduled source refreshes and answering read services; cost as conversion with the group normal at every reader less conversion as served; several-group cost as the sum; and tier one at quarterly cost >= 0.05 pp.
Read feature_registry.csv, feature_store.yaml and search_pipeline.yaml. They establish the seven groups and owners; batch/stream validity hours (stock 2 h, price 36 h, trend 6 h, seller 120 h); request-time promise, history and match; the two-pass ranker (rank: match, price, trend, seller; rerank per request: stock, promise, history); and the render readers (promise badge, price label, history strip). The page cache sits after rank and before rerank/render, reads every request, writes only non-override requests, and its TTL rose from 5 to 12 minutes on 9 June 2026. This fixed which groups are cache-diluted (rank only) and which are not (rerank and render).
Read markets.csv (ISO-2 to mart ISO-3 keys), serving_regions.csv (user hash split, two price-replication regions) and feature_source_traffic_2026.xlsx (chain shares by market, price-source shares by market, click partition session shares). These are the exposure weights; chain shares sum to 1 per market.
Inspected every remaining file, then chose inputs. Used: mart_search_conversion_daily_2026.csv (273 dates x 6 markets x 2 platforms x 2 regions = 6,552 rows; Q3 123,019,841 sessions and 14,468,451 attributed orders, served conversion 11.761071%); edge_search_sessions_hourly_2026.csv (reconciles exactly to the mart for every date x market x device; Q3 26,496 market-device-hour rows); edge_page_cache_daily_2026.csv; component_render_hourly_2026.csv (235,872 rows); feature_status_hourly_2026.csv (339,465 rows); chain_feed_gaps_2026.csv (1,633 gaps); competitor_price_batch_runs_2026.csv (1,365 source-days); clickstream_lag_episodes_2026.csv (105 episodes); seller_score_runs_2026.csv (889,961 runs, 8,000 sellers); seller_search_traffic_monthly_2026.csv (72,000 rows); and experiment_platform_export_2026.sqlite (67 experiments, 24,240 daily arm-rows). Not used as cost evidence: ranker_offline_replay_2026q3.xlsx and ranker_v7_feature_importance.csv, because NDCG/MRR/gain deltas are ranking relevance, not conversion; ranker_release_log_2026.txt (releases state no feature change) and incident_and_change_register_2026.xlsx were used only as context/cross-check, not as exposure, because incident duration is not service effect; serving_regions.csv mattered only to confirm Q3 price replication was uniform; promo_calendar_2026.csv was not needed because experiments are stratified daily and the cost target is actual Q3 exposure, not a promo-adjusted baseline; feature_status_hourly source health was never treated as feature state without validity.
Scoped populations and windows: all 92 Q3 dates, six markets, APP and WEB, both regions, edge/mart sessions as the decision population; January-September experiments as the effect evidence; Q3 service state as control-arm degradation; monthly grain only for seller traffic and for the requested reporting.
Reconstructed actual feature absence rather than using source health. Stock: intersected each gap with its market chain shares and applied 2 h validity, so only the post-expiry remainder counts (Q3 session-weighted absence 0.035539; raw gap time would have been 0.102027). Trend: lag episode plus 6 h validity (Q3 0.083887 raw, 0.083887 to 0.032695 after cache). Price: per source and region, absence begins 36 h after the last successful load and ends at the next success (Q3 0.105886 raw). Seller: per day from each seller's last successful run, presence for 120 h (days 0-4), comparing actual service with a counterfactual where every run succeeds, weighted by monthly top-10 impressions (totals reconcile to 10 x search sessions). July/August/September excess-absent shares were 0.009448 / 0.017535 / 0.014800; run failure rates were 17.4% / 29.1% / 23.7%, which raw use would have misread as exposure. Match: replica outage seconds directly. History/promise: request error fraction unhealthy/observed directly.
Independently checked render suppression against those constructions. Price label suppression is 0.020030 + 1.000238 x predicted absence (residual about 0.0028), and it jumps at 16:00, exactly 36 h after the 04:00 batch; e.g. July 8 crawl-de-at failure produces excess from 16:00 that day, and July 9 adds benelux from 16:00. History and promise suppression is approximately 0.0200 + status error fraction. This validated the validity model and showed the ~2% baseline suppression is not degradation cost.
Turned experiments into full-strength effects. Pivoted daily market-device arms into cells (10,584 completed feature-ablation cells and 1,512 holdout cells; the 24 cells of aborted trend EXP-2663 were excluded). Per cell: risk difference held minus control in pp; exposure = (1 - control degraded fraction) x origin fraction for rank groups, or (1 - control degraded fraction) for rerank; origin = built_at_origin / result_page_views for that date x market x device. Control degradation uses actual Q3 service state, so raw Q3 ablation deltas, which are diluted by both cache and control outages, were not used directly. Combined cells by precision-weighted least squares through the origin, no intercept, weight 1/variance of the risk difference.

Assumptions and judgment calls.

Stock gaps were taken from feed_stopped_at/feed_resumed_at converted to UTC, with validity expiry at gap start + 2 h and recovery at resume; the 15-minute sync interval was not padded onto recovery because no per-chain last-sync timestamps exist. Padding 15 minutes raises stock to 0.102239 pp; ignoring validity entirely gives 0.265137 pp. Both leave stock on the list, so no reasonable choice changes the call, though they change its magnitude.
Trend cost starts 6 h after lag onset and ends at catch-up. Peak-lag semantics are ambiguous from the file; I used episodes delayed by 6 h, and sensitivity is 0.021661 pp (lag-remainder), 0.028927-0.028957 pp (6 h delay / peak-filtered), 0.032031 (peak-filtered whole episode), 0.032277 (no validity). All are below 0.05, so the trend call is insensitive, though its exact value is not.
Seller presence was evaluated at daily effective-date granularity with age <= 4 days counted valid (120 h), and cost is excess absence (normal-present but actual-absent), not all actual absence, because some sellers are stale even in normal service; cost is weighted by top-10 result impressions because that is the exposure the ranker's seller features actually act on. Treating failed runs as feature loss would misprice an incident whose scores usually remain valid for 5 days.
Page-cache hits were treated as not exposing the current rank service: only origin-built requests incur the rank effect, at daily market-device origin shares. The alternative (a hit serving a page built during degradation, or showing pre-outage normal pages mid-outage) cannot be reconstructed from daily aggregates; it would mainly raise rank-group costs. Under the stated model this matters decisively for trend: without cache dilution its cost would be 0.074297 pp and it would cross the line; with the pipeline's actual cache placement it is 0.028957 pp.
Feature and component effects were treated as additive per reader and constant over January-September, pooled for precision; monthly releases change no features. Q3-only pooling is reported as sensitivity and does not alter any call.
Roughly 2% baseline render suppression is normal service, not degradation cost; component effects are per rendered eligible session so excess suppression, not raw suppression, is charged.
Render reads at request time and after the cache, so badge/label/strip costs are not origin-diluted; stock/promise/history rerank per request and are not diluted.
Q3 price source replication statuses agreed across ams1/fra2, so region split is immaterial to Q3 cost; April not-replicated nights only affected experiment control adjustment.
The cost of all groups together is the charter section 3.4 sum; it is not a claim that joint degradation is linear in a physical sense, only the charter's definition.
Point estimates are modelled counterfactuals, not exact certainties; approximate 95% sampling-only intervals are on sheet 02 (e.g. promise 0.064218-0.070230, trend 0.026563-0.031352, history 0.028055-0.029754) and exclude validity/exposure-model uncertainty.
A different reasonable choice on any of the above does not move price, stock, promise, seller or match across the line. Trend's call flips only if one also discards cache dilution; history's flips only if one applies the line to August rather than the quarter, which the charter forbids.
