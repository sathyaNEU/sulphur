Assumptions and judgment calls.

Weights. Chose Sep 21, 2026 because Sec.3.1 says latest quarter-end snapshot at the assessment date and Dec 21 had not closed. December 2027 plan weights are disclosed; they move Labels to 9.99, Noise 9.10, Data 6.49, Releases 6.39, Retunes 4.47 and change no band.
Observed adoption as the pinning gate. “Actually run” is read as first production refit using the option, evidenced by scheduler options, not calendar completion. Three historical routes per family are used directly; no delay-function fit. A component-weighted model was considered (roughly 78% of variance) but rejected because family routes are directly observed and the record does not settle a functional form; sensitivity stays inside the stated release range. If the committee instead defines pinned as “option released to environment,” releases move to 4.43 and Monitor - that is a different operating definition, not the scheduler evidence.
Noise increment. Half replicate-pair MS per warm-started refit, 13-fold accumulation. Reasonable alternative: treat pair spread as one refit’s movement, giving noise 12.87; still Escalation and does not change the call.
Label treatment. Raw label-freeze shadow MS, not net of replicate spread. Shadow event regressions reproduce release/retune effects as instrumented, and combined label-plus-noise MS is invariant under a replicate reallocation. If labels were instead netted against half the replicate pair, label movement would be 4.11 (Monitor) and noise 12.87 (Escalation); that reading is not used because it reallocates two independently instrumented sources and is not what the shadow estimand records.
Independence/additivity. Weekly increments and sources accumulate linearly in MS; source effects persist once landed; no cross-source covariance is estimable or assumed. A strong positive/negative covariance structure would change year RMS but is not identified by these instruments.
Panel forecast supplements. Tier model assumes zero-mean Gaussian change with variance equal to total expected MS; panel held at Sep 21, 2026; share pooled across panel merchants. It is indicative Schedule C reporting, not a Sec.4 placement input. Different distribution/panel evolution could move tier percentages, not source placements.
Alternatives rejected.
Calendar PCB as adoption. Plan dates give 676/92.86% and releases 4.00 Monitor; slip-only gives 648/89.01% and releases 4.43 Monitor. Rejected because all 42 historical first-use dates lag the calendar, often by months; standard Sec.1.5 keys on when the family trains/scores on pinned views. This evidence points against a Monitor release call and is why observed adoption is primary.
Release heterogeneity by group/count/drift. Rejected: within-family release MS CV is 2.4-8.2% across 11-20 pairs and residuals show no group, group-count or PSI trend; PSI alerts (38 of 1,512 weeks, max 0.2252) are not Sec.1.2 sources.
Complexity/performance as placement inputs. Registry epochs/components explain family noise/bootstrap differences but are already embedded in instrument MS; compute hours/nodes had no adoption-lag relation beyond components; AUC/calibration/brier are not stability dimensions; owner shows inconsistent, weak lag grouping. None overrides measured effects.
Cold-start/historical regime pooling. 2024-2025 mixed fp32/bf16, cold/warm and 26/52/104-week windows; using them would contaminate the 2027 warm-bf16, late-2026-window regime the instruments measure.
Pooled merchant micro-RMS or mean of pair RMS. Rejected in favor of Sec.3.1 family weighting and Sec.3.2 root-of-mean-of-pair-MS. Dec 2027 weights and raw bootstrap are disclosed sensitivities, neither changes a band.
Retune placement. Retunes at 4.47 are close to but below 5.5; no evidence supports escalating them. If the committee wants an additional buffer, that is a policy choice beyond Sec.4, not a data finding.
