Turned source health into serving/freshness exposure, not raw incident time. Stock gap timestamps at +01:00 were converted to UTC and features treated as expired from feed-stop + 2 h until resume (chain market shares from the traffic workbook; overlapping windows merged). Assumptions and judgment calls.

Partial-source/partition/entity effects scale with the traffic/source share of missing features. Chosen because the traffic workbook provides exactly those weights and component suppression fits confirm share × exposure behavior; true ranking effects need not be perfectly linear, but this is the only identified scaling and cost differences were small. A nonlinearity large enough to flip would most plausibly affect broad-source price/trend shocks; no data supported one.
Cost is additive across groups per Sec. 3.4, not a joint-intervention causal estimate; simultaneous degradation interaction is out of scope by charter definition.
Trend lag ramps 1:1 to peak then drains linearly. Peak timing is not in the episode file, so this is the natural reconstruction; treating full >6 h episodes as stale instead (status-style) barely moves estimates because episodes are hours-to-days and validity edges are short; no tier changes. Peak ≤6 h episodes cost zero even though status counts them.
Stock stale window starts at stop + 2 h, ignoring up-to-15-minute sync phase; effect < a quarter-hour per gap and immaterial to 0.05 pp decisions.
Seller normal service means every scheduled run succeeds, scores live 120 h, and search impact scales with top10-result impressions, not unweighted runs; inactive-seller natural expiration is excluded because it would expire in both actual and counterfactual. This is why raw status severity does not imply list cost.
Price cost is region/source-share weighted with April replication ignored for Q3; Q3 regions had identical statuses, so this does not drive results.
Primary effects are all-period, structure-selected, assuming stability after cache/exposure adjustment; month terms did not improve weighted fit. Q3-only refit was retained as sensitivity; it changes values (trend 0.068638 → 0.056406, still over; price 0.165720 → 0.176118) but no tier.
Structure selection used weighted residual AIC, with stock market+device and most others device; full market×device was considered and not selected because it did not improve AIC enough. Constant-effect pooling was retained as sensitivity.
Actual cache state is not simulated request-by-request; daily origin fractions were used to de-dilute experiments, and steady-state Q3 degradation is treated as fully exposed (cache holds pages built during degradation), smoothing only transitions. A different cache-age distribution changes costs below the line spacing, not tiers.
Business dates include weekends because Sec. 3.3 says all search sessions; filtering to weekdays would understate exposure and is not what the mart business-date field instructs.
Reasonable alternatives above do not flip any listed/unlisted group; the only genuinely close call is trend under Q3-only refit at 0.056406 pp, still above 0.05, and its September-alone refit is below, but Sec. 3.4/4.1 evaluate the quarter, not a month.
Alternatives rejected.

Call history tier one from a bad month: August is 0.067436 pp, over the line, but July 0.003842 and September 0.014947 make the quarter 0.028827 pp; Sec. 4.1 is quarter-based, and July's pre-reshard period is part of the quarter. This is evidence against listing history; it does not survive the required averaging window.

Offline quality does not price live degraded exposure.
Exclude component suppression as "not ranking": that would leave promise at 0.031885 pp, below the line, but Sec. 3.3 explicitly prices normal service to every service that reads it; the badge contributes 0.034692 pp, making promise 0.066577 pp, and price's label contributes 0.048980 pp of its 0.165720 pp. Rank-only would wrongly drop promise, so it is rejected.
Use raw feature-status unhealthy seconds/requests as cost exposure: it starts at gap/batch failure rather than validity expiry and would overstate every freshness group; the 12 July price label rows confirm actual label suppression only from validity expiry 16:00 to next load 04:00, and stock/trend have analogous windows.
