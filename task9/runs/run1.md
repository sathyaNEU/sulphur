4. Assumptions and judgment calls.
Dec 2027 planner book is the section 3.1 latest quarter-end snapshot for a 2027 assessment, because the plan explicitly supplies it and no Dec 2027 actual book exists. Sep 2026 alternative is disclosed and changes no placement.
Opening snapshot is 21 Dec 2026 (Schedule C 2026), and the 52 assessment refreshes are 28 Dec 2026 through 20 Dec 2027 inclusive; excluding the opening refresh follows section 2.2's "belonging to the four snapshot pairs."
Refit randomness accumulates linearly over 13 warm-started refreshes; each source's weekly mean-squares add. This is the reading most consistent with section 1.4 and the panel lag behavior, but it is a model, not a contractual formula. A non-accumulating reading would cut noise roughly by sqrt(13) and could change its band; I reject it for 2027 warm-start conditions.
Training-data movement is realized window weight change, not a bootstrap resample of 2027 data, because section 1.2 defines the source as rows actually trained on; K merely transfers instrument sensitivity to that change. Bootstrap halving (B-R)/2 treats replicate and bootstrap pairs as two-refit comparisons.
Event effects are independent mean-square contributions; instruments carry no sign/cross-term, and repeated same-group releases have no special rule. If releases are correlated within group, actual combined could differ though each source's placement would likely not.
Adoption forecast treats historical rollout behavior as predictive and counts temporary rollback weeks probabilistically. It is the least certain input. Different reasonable rollout rules move pinned share 32.143-38.187% (or 83.379% if the roadmap is trusted) and release movement 1.974-4.717, but none flips a band; this is why the call is stated as robust rather than pin-dependent.
Adoption curves pool only three changes per family, so family-level pinned weeks are expectations with substantial uncertainty; the aggregate share is more stable than any family value.
Label revisions are steady-state weekly effects carried over 13 refreshes because no 2027 late-revision schedule exists beyond the labelled-row plan.
Sep 21 2026 is the covenant panel base because that is the latest closed quarter-end snapshot in the records; latest Nov 23 panel is a disclosed sensitivity. Tier changes are expected values under Gaussian centred deltas, not a simulated realized book.
HLTH instruments restricted to bf16/warm-start because 2027 production is warm/bf16 and early 2026 mixed-regime pairs would contaminate constants; impact is below displayed precision.
Duplicate-load resolution to highest suffix is inferred, not documented; exact reproduction of all monitor rates is the evidence for it.
None of the defensible alternatives above changes the three non-monitor calls; the pin-adoption judgment changes only the release estimate within Monitor. Misreading warm-start accumulation is the one judgment that could flip noise, and I consider the standard/panel evidence strong against that reading.
