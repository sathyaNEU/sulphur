Training-data pair movement is the filed interval’s mean per-refit sensitivity, not a 13-week compounded variance. Pinning is read as affecting feature-release reach only (§1.5), not windows, rows or refit cadence.
Feature-group identity is not separately modelled; family instrument levels were stable across release groups, so releases use family MS.
Merchants’ own change is reported apart per §5.2 as a global book-weighted residual over the three 2026 closed pairs, not decomposed by family or carried into any source band.
