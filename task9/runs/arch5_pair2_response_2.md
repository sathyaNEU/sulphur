Training-data definition. For refresh t with window size N and rows added A / dropped D, turnover fraction f_t = (A + D) / (2N). Expected data movement MS for that refresh = f_t x (bootstrap pair MS - replicate pair MS), because the bootstrap pair difference contains both resampling sensitivity and seed noise; subtracting replicate isolates the training-data component. I used filed instruments rather than panel-fitted f-slopes, because small-window families were noisy; mid/large-N panel regressions tracked the instrument.
Warm-start accumulation, the deciding modelling choice. Standard 1.4 says a warm-started refit starts from prior weights, so earlier refit randomness carries into later refits. I therefore accumulated each week's source movement across a 13-refresh pair (random-walk carry): pair source MS = sum over the 13 refreshes of per-refresh source MS; an event contributes its pair MS in the landing week. I did not impose an AR/decay/forgetting coefficient because no such parameter is filed anywhere and panel change autocorrelations were not identifiable at this panel size. The alternative - treating weekly snapshots as independent, standard for cold refits - directly contradicts 1.4 and was rejected; supporting evidence is below.

Assumptions and judgment calls.

Assessment date taken as 24 November 2026, records through the 23 November weekly refresh; standard requires a November assessment on records to the latest refresh. Effect of a few days either way: none on 2027 inputs.
Warm-start treated as full carry/random-walk accumulation, with no decay. This is the only reading supported by 1.4 and by the flat 2026 own residuals. A genuinely fitted forgetting factor is not identifiable here; if one existed and materially discounted multi-week carry, every weekly source movement would shrink, releases/pinning logic would be unchanged, but data could fall out of Plan and optimisation/labels could fall below escalation - so this is the assumption most capable of changing the recommendation, and I flag rather than hide it.
MRM pair instruments treated as constant per family across 2027 and read directly as per-refresh movement; release effects are family means, not per-release-group severity. Per-release severity was not filed and is not inferable from PSI or release groups; using pair instruments is the standard-consistent measurement.
Panel treated as fixed at 21 September 2026 membership for 2027 expectations; attrition after that date is unknown and Schedule C4.1 does not replace leavers. A shrinking panel changes counts, not per-merchant probabilities or the cap verdict.
Pinned weeks taken on schedule and gating at the pin Monday inclusive; if rollout slips, releases must be re-banded before filing (to Escalation at the 10.269-point slip case), so the Monitor placement is conditional, not unconditional.
Gaussian, zero-mean tier change; score distribution taken at 21 September 2026 for all four pairs; families pooled unweighted as the covenant panel reads. Reasonable alternatives move the headline by hundredths to a few tenths (aggregate-own sensitivity 13.398 / 6.840), not the cap verdict; the counterfactual removal is an upper-bound governance construct per 6.1, not a causal plan-delivery model.
Own residuals allowed to be slightly negative in single family-quarters and carried as three-pair averages; clamping at zero would raise own only marginally and would not move any placement.
Calendar-2027 pinned share used as the headline because the ask is "2027's weekly retrains"; the snapshot-pair counting variant is shown alongside rather than substituted.
December 2027 plan Book not used for 3.1 weights; it does not change placements.
