Assumptions and judgment calls.

Latest quarter-end snapshot = 21 Sep 2026, the latest snapshot closed at the 24 Nov 2026 assessment date (records to 23 Nov); the standard’s “latest quarter-end snapshot” therefore means that, not a future/Dec 2027 book. Rationale: assessment is made in November of the year before on current records. Using Dec 2027 instead changes exact shares slightly but no placement and not the call (shown above).
Assessment date labelled 24 November 2026, the day after the latest weekly refresh; the prompt does not name a date. This does not affect any figure, only the deck/workbook headers.
Merchants’ own change is carried as the 2026 residual, not re-forecast, exactly per §5.2; family residuals are used as they stand even where a single pair is negative (e.g. DINE pair 1), because §5.2 does not authorize truncation. Averaging pairs equally rather than weighting by pair panel counts matches §3.2’s equal pair treatment; book-weighting families matches §3.1.
Per-refresh instrument mean-squares are accumulated over 13 refreshes. Releases/retunes/labels were checked persistent; noise and training are treated as accruing per refresh under the warm-start regime the 2027 assessment will begin in. No source-specific persistence beyond that is observable or imposed. If noise in fact mean-reverts strongly rather than accumulating, its points and the covenant projection would fall and the tier result would be less severe; the placement of releases/labels/training/retunes would not thereby become Monitor (releases and labels rest on instrument movement alone), but I flag this as the least directly identified dynamic. The workbook Basis sheet states this scope explicitly.
Training-data movement is sliding-window turnover, not a generic “less data = more movement” scaling. The filed plan’s row dip therefore quiets Pair 2 rather than amplifying it; this is the reading the window/rows records support. A cruder size-only scaling would move training points but cannot move it out of the 5.5-8.0 band by enough to change the call given its pair range (5.81-7.38).
Tier expectation holds the 21 Sep 2026 panel score distribution fixed for all four 2027 pairs, treats changes as Gaussian with mean zero and pair-movement variance, pools by panel counts, and averages the four pair shares with equal weight. Real 2027 score drift/attrition is unknowable at assessment; this is the committed-estimate reading. The counterfactual is the standard’s full removal of placed sources, not a forecast of a particular remediation.
Planned ISO week is the first pinned retrain, inclusive, and “2027 weekly retrains” means calendar 2027 (52 refreshes × 14 families). Counting only the 52 assessment-window refreshes from 28 Dec 2026 through 20 Dec 2027 instead gives 662/728 = 90.93% (stated in the workbook and Slide 6 notes); it does not touch the stability call.
Release mean-squares are family instrument means regardless of how many feature groups a release touches (2026 instruments vary little by group count); multiple releases to one family in one refresh would be additive (none occur).
