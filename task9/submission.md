## Step-by-Step

1. Assemble the record. The ten quarterly panel files keep the later load where a refresh was re-sent and map Tolka Pay's re-keyed merchants through the migration file, which leaves 29,580 panel merchants (34,148 identifiers before the mapping) and 3,360,865 scored merchant-weeks; the registry's family codes are matched to the scheduler's; the manifests' 2,473 lines carry 2,464 distinct runs, 1,764 of them production refits; the pair register holds 644 pairs (84 replicate, 56 bootstrap, 168 retune shadow, 224 release shadow, 112 label freeze).
2. Retunes and label revisions. Each instrument's pair variance by family, book weighted at the 21 September 2026 snapshot, scaled by what a snapshot pair holds (a retune every fourth week, a label revision every week): retunes 4.47 points and label revisions 9.98. Every route lands these two; they are not where the call is decided.
3. Training data. The bootstrap pairs differ by 259.89 points squared, book weighted (247.16 net of the replicate pairs' seed part). Read as the movement, that is 15.72 points and escalation. But a bootstrap pair resamples the whole window, while two quarter-end models share every row of their windows except the thirteen weeks that left and the thirteen that entered: the bootstrap spread gives the noise per training row, and the manifests' windows (6 families on 104 weeks, the rest on 52) and the warehouse and plan rows give how many rows each 2027 snapshot pair turns over. Training data alone is 6.62 points, a remediation plan.
4. Optimisation noise. The replicate pairs differ by 12.73 points squared, book weighted, which is 3.57 points and monitor if a refit's fresh noise is all two snapshot models differ by. But each weekly model starts from the previous week's weights, so the noise persists and builds over the thirteen refits between snapshots. The score history's mean-square change by gap, over each family's settled warm weeks, bends towards a ceiling; a saturating term tied to the replicate pairs gives a persistence of 0.79 to 0.95 by family and 6.89 points, a remediation plan. Fitted free of the replicate pairs it gives 8.77 points but implies replicate pairs of 17.44 where 12.73 is measured; a straight line through gaps 8 to 26 starts at 5.96 while the curve is still bending; thirteen refits each adding a whole replicate pair's variance gives 12.87.
5. Pinned feature views and feature releases. On the calendar's planned weeks 90.9 per cent of 2027's retrains run on pinned views, and 87.1 per cent on its completed dates. The calendar marks a change complete when the image is released; the manifests show when each family first trained on the three past changes: 42 family rollouts, first use 3 to 31 weeks after completion (median 14), a median of 6.3 weeks with no custom training components and 28.8 with 5, and 4.4 per cent of the following 26 weeks reverted. A lag model on custom components carries this to the pinned views: 55.0 per cent of 2027's retrains. Leaving each past rollout out in turn, the model predicts its first 39 weeks on at 56.2, 57.5 and 56.1 per cent against 58.8, 56.6 and 59.0 recorded. A roadmap release moves a family's scores only in the weeks it is not on pinned views: with the release shadows' variance and the roadmap's 24 releases, feature releases alone are 6.65 points, a remediation plan (4.00 on the calendar's dates, monitor; 9.67 if 2026's 22 releases are carried instead of the roadmap).
6. Shares. The record's mean-square change over thirteen refreshes in the settled weeks is 509.15, book weighted; less the model-side sources as they stood in 2026 it leaves merchants' own change at 43.9 per cent of all movement (43.7 on the 2026 snapshot pairs alone). Each source's share of model-side movement is in answer 1. Reading each instrument as it stands puts merchants' own change at 14.2 per cent and training data at 62.5 per cent of model-side movement, and the thirteen-week total still closes, so the closing total checks nothing. Taking every thirteen-week gap from July 2024 puts merchants' own share at 49.2, but before each family's settled weeks the manifests show cold retrains on a 26-week window and the calendar's three rollouts, so the record's change there runs 1.04 to 2.01 times the settled weeks' and is not the 2026 sources' movement.
7. The check. Each route's own model of how a source's variance grows with the gap is asked for the weekly score-change variance of the settled weeks, which the record holds at 42.34: this assessment gives 42.23; both the bootstrap and the replicate pairs read at face value give 279.06; the bootstrap spread at any gap 270.43; the replicate pairs at any gap 50.92.
8. Tier changes. The 2026 snapshot pairs ran at 14.8 per cent on the monitoring panel.
