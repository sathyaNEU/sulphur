## Step-by-Step

1. Assemble the record. The ten quarterly panel files keep the later load where a refresh was re-sent and map Tolka Pay's re-keyed merchants through the migration file, which leaves 29,580 panel merchants (34,148 identifiers before the mapping) and 3,360,865 scored merchant-weeks; the registry's family codes are matched to the scheduler's; the manifests' 2,473 lines carry 2,464 distinct runs, 1,764 of them production refits; the pair register holds 644 pairs (84 replicate, 56 bootstrap, 168 retune shadow, 224 release shadow, 112 label freeze).
2. Retunes and label revisions. Each instrument's pair variance by family, book weighted at the 21 September 2026 snapshot, scaled by what a snapshot pair holds (a retune every fourth week, a label revision every week): retunes 4.47 points and label revisions 9.98. Every route lands these two; they are not where the call is decided.
3. Training data. The bootstrap pairs differ by 259.89 points squared, book weighted (247.16 net of the replicate pairs' seed part). Read as the movement, that is 15.72 points and escalation. But a bootstrap pair resamples the whole window, while two quarter-end models share every row of their windows except the thirteen weeks that left and the thirteen that entered: the bootstrap spread gives the noise per training row, and the manifests' windows (6 families on 104 weeks, the rest on 52) and the warehouse and plan rows give how many rows each 2027 snapshot pair turns over, the change in a window fit between two snapshots carrying the per-row noise times 1/N1 + 1/N2 - 2S/(N1 N2), with N1 and N2 the rows in the two windows and S the rows they share. Training data alone is 6.62 points, a remediation plan.
4. Optimisation noise. The replicate pairs differ by 12.73 points squared, book weighted, which would be 3.57 points and monitor if a refit's fresh noise were all two snapshot models differ by; the standard's section 1.4 rules that out, since a warm-started refit carries the randomness of the refits before it, so the later snapshot model holds the noise of the thirteen refits between. Added up in full, each refit's fresh draw (half a replicate pair) gives 9.10 points and escalation, and a whole replicate pair a refit 12.87; but the carried noise decays. Each family's settled warm weeks run from thirteen weeks after its last cold-started retrain in the manifests, and not before the refresh of 6 October 2025 (the record's last 60 weeks). Over them the score history's mean-square change by gap h bends towards a ceiling: fitted family by family over gaps 1 to 26 as b h + A(1 - p^h), with A(1 - p^2) set to the family's replicate-pair variance, each gap weighted by the inverse square of its mean-square change and p searched from 0.50 to 0.985 in steps of 0.0025, it gives a persistence p of 0.79 to 0.95 by family and 6.89 points, a remediation plan. The full sums are refused by the bend: a persistence of 1 puts every source on a straight line through the origin, but every family's line through gaps 8 to 26 meets gap 0 above zero (35.6 points squared, book weighted, and at least 14.3 in every family), and a line through the origin misfits every family's curve, by the same weights, 3.4 times or more as badly as the fitted curve (34 times at the median family). Fitted free of the replicate pairs the curve gives 8.77 points but implies replicate pairs of 17.44 where 12.73 is measured, so it is refused by the pairs. A straight line's start moves with its first gap (4.45 points from gap 1, 5.96 from gap 8, 7.50 from gap 20), so the curve is still bending, and the fitted curve put through the same line on gaps 8 to 26 returns 5.50 against its own 6.89: a line's start is not the movement at thirteen weeks.
5. Pinned feature views and feature releases. 2027's retrains are the 52 weekly refreshes of the four snapshot pairs closing in 2027, 28 December 2026 to 20 December 2027, and the share asked for is of those expected to actually run on pinned views. On the calendar's planned weeks 90.9 per cent of them would, and 87.1 per cent on its completed dates. The calendar marks a change complete when the image is released; the manifests show when each family first trained on the three past changes: 42 family rollouts, first use 3 to 31 weeks after completion (median 14), a median of 6.3 weeks with no custom training components and 28.8 with 5, and 4.4 per cent of the following 26 weeks reverted. A lag model carries this to the pinned views: each family's 2027 rollout is placed at its planned week plus the past rollouts' mean slip, counted in weekly refreshes from the planned week to the refresh week of the completed date (1.21 weeks); the lag from that week to first use is fitted log-linear on custom components with its spread integrated, and the reverted share is netted from the weeks on, which puts 55.0 per cent of 2027's retrains on pinned views; every take-up form, log-linear or linear, by group, by family or pooled, integrated or as a plug-in median lag, with or without reverts, puts it between 54.4 and 59.6 per cent. Leaving each past rollout out in turn, the model puts 56.2, 57.5 and 56.1 per cent of its first 39 weeks, counted from each family's completed date, on the change, against 58.8, 56.6 and 59.0 recorded. Under the standard's section 1.5 no feature release moves a family's scores in the weeks it trains and scores on pinned views: with the release shadows' variance and the roadmap's 24 releases, feature releases alone are 6.65 points, a remediation plan (4.00 on the calendar's planned weeks and 4.46 on its completed dates, monitor either way; 10.27 left whole on pinned weeks, which section 1.5 refuses; 9.67 if the release level of the settled weeks is carried instead of the roadmap, which section 2.1 refuses).
6. The check. Each route's own model of how a source's variance grows with the gap is asked for the mean-square change between consecutive weekly refreshes over the same settled weeks, which the record holds at 42.34, book weighted: this assessment gives 42.23; both the bootstrap and the replicate pairs read at face value give 279.06; the bootstrap spread at any gap 270.43; the replicate pairs at any gap 50.92.

No other conclusion survives. 56 variants (the gap fit over 20, 26 or 30 gaps, weighted or flat, pooled or per family, events taken out, on 2026 only; the settled weeks started at the first warm retrain, thirteen weeks after the last cold one with no floor, or at the last past rollout's first use; the take-up model log-linear, linear, by group or by family, integrated or as a plug-in median lag, and pooled or without reverts; the planned book, the latest bootstrap week, four tier mappings, and the panel's weights in place of the book's) file the same five bands, the nearest a variant comes to a line being optimisation noise at 6.15 against 5.5. The two decisive steps are independent: with only the training-data step missed, training data goes to escalation (15.72) and nothing else moves; with only the optimisation step missed, optimisation noise goes to escalation (9.10) and nothing else moves; with both missed both move (training data 15.72 and optimisation noise 9.10), the hurried call in the Final Recommendation. The pinned-views take-up supports the call: with the calendar's dates taken for first use, feature releases drop to monitor (4.00 on its planned weeks, 4.46 on its completed dates). The thinnest margin is optimisation noise's floor: its band starts at 6.05, between the flat fit on settled weeks from thirteen weeks after the last cold retrain with no floor (6.15) and the line through gaps 8 to 26 (5.96).
